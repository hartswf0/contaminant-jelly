CONTAMINANT · JAR LAB

three ways to bottle a film

Every jar is its own: its tint, its fill line, its tag, its number. The lid is a reel; the label says CONTAMINANT. Hover a jar and the film fills it, up to its line; then the game it became. Click to swim it.
