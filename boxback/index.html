Corrugated Sky · jelly cut

2:26 · the Jelly Orchestra plays the song · a red Afterlight Medusa carries the memories

The first jelly cut. Memories from public-domain archive films travel as flecks and bloom as full pictures. At the gate, a painted girl and dog stream into the shadow dog. It closes on a boy and a dog.

critic round 5: reference stronger

An independent critic says it is not yet its own work. Fire and rain don't read, the blooms are small, and it plays like a slideshow. 5 of 9 memories read cold. Published as an experiment, with the next repair listed on its operator page.
