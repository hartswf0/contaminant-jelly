CONTAMINANT · jelly-films · Handmade

A jellyfish of film swims an archive. Every film you pass is a cloud of light. Come near it, light it or pulse, and it clears into what it shows. Take a frame and it becomes one of your tentacles.

This trailer is made the video-to-game way: each moment of a film is cut against the moment in the game that moves like it.

PLAY (free, in the browser, phone or desktop): https://hartswf0.github.io/contaminant-jelly/
• Hold to swim · Tap a film to take it · Hold on it to see it clearly · Double-tap to pulse · Outrun the angler
• H hides every word · ?trailer lets it swim itself
