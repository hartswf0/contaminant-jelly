Rise through the second-hand archive: a tube of 109 films, each ring a moment. Hold where you want to go and tap to pulse. Brush the walls and they play. Linger, and a frame joins one of your tentacles. Each tentacle is a film strip, and each new film grows a new one.

Stop pulsing and you sink, back through time. The red ink burns what it touches; two fast pulses shake it off. How it works · first version · Contaminant Jelly
