TOURKEY TROUTTOWN · FOR THE GROWN-UP IN THE ROOM

Run it in 40 minutes

Print

  1. This page. Once. It is yours.
  2. The next two pages (the Porthole Log). Double-sided. One sheet per child.
  3. slips.pdf. One page for every 6 children. 21 children: print pages 1 to 4. Cut on the dashed lines.

Bring

  1. One die for every 3 children.
  2. Pencils. Crayons if you have them.
  3. A screen with sound for the film.

Do not print

  1. Anything else. Three files exist; you need this one and the slips.

Seat

  1. Groups of 3 in a circle. A group of 4 works too.
  2. Hand each child one slip and one log.

Run

ClockYou doChildren do
0 to 3 minPlay the film from the start. Stop at 3:05.Watch. The film reads every step aloud.
3 to 9Say: “Front page. Zones 1, 2, 3.”Copy the slip into zone 1. Draw in the ring. Write 3 lines.
9 to 15Say: “Pass your paper left.” Then: “Zone 5.”Read the slip that came with the paper. Pick a button, draw it. Write endings 1, 2, 3.
15 to 21Say: “Pass left again. Zone 6.”Pick a button, draw it. Write endings 4, 5, 6.
21 to 27Say: “Give it back. Roll once. Zones 7 and 8.”Roll. Circle the die. Write the gossip and the message home.
27 to 40Play the film from 3:05. Stop after the highest slip number you handed out.Stand when your number plays. Read your rolled ending aloud.

Rules

  1. The slip travels with the paper. Each friend reads the choices from the slip.
  2. Any ending counts if it has one action and one thing you can see.
  3. A child who cannot write draws or tells a partner, who writes.
  4. Out of time: skip minute 27 onward. The logs are complete without it.
