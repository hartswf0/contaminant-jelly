## 1. Chapter premise: language models make stronger films by compiling rules and code than by guessing pixels

Public and industrial debates around "AI filmmaking" suffer from a category mistake. Commercial text-to-video platforms frame cinema as statistical next-frame pixel prediction: a user types a short descriptive prompt into a diffusion transformer, and the network interpolates plausible surface textures across a 5-to-10-second clip. As analyzed in *Worldtext and Same-Worldness*, pixel-prediction video collapses under the anomaly of return. Turn the camera away and back again, alter one character's hidden motive, or run twelve sequential scenes in the same room, and walls drift, props vanish, and outputs regress toward corpus averages ("AI SPAM"; cf. Shumailov et al. 2024).

This chapter asks a different question: **how does a language model make a film when it is forbidden from guessing pixels?**

Across the PAELEO and Trout Town studio corpus, language models make films through a text-to-code-to-video and text-to-cutout-to-multiplane pipeline. Extending Hannes Bajohr's operative ekphrasis and Peter Naur's "Programming as Theory Building", natural language acts as a constitutional prompt compiler (SCION), not as a caption for a latent denoiser. Under SCION, language models write deterministic 2D Canvas/SVG geometry, assemble open-license archival photographs and handmade paper cutouts across seven-layer virtual multiplane rigs, enforce formal constraints from the Oulipo and its sister Ouxpo workshops, and submit rendered playback to quarantined, fresh-context blind critics empowered to fail any cut whose governing rule is invisible to an unbriefed viewer.

| Operational dimension | Commercial pixel-diffusion video ("AI SPAM") | SCION constitutional filmmaking (worldtext, minimum viable video-text) |
|---|---|---|
| Role of the language model | Expands thin captions into dense style adjectives for a black-box denoiser | Compiles a creative thesis into executable Canvas/SVG code, multiplane exposure sheets, state transitions and independent verification tests |
| Atomic unit of cinema | Unstructured pixel frames with no internal objecthood | Bounded 8 to 10 s patches, kinematic rigs (plane 0 to plane 6), stateful modules (`deeds/`, `trans/`, `conseq/`) |
| Material ontology | Uncredited scraped training-set emulsion | Foraged or handmade, never faked: credited open-license archival prints (FSA/OWI), cut paper, procedural vector rigs, scanned or redrawn student worksheets |
| Source of narrative surprise | Stochastic hallucination and prompt drift | Formal anti-chance (lipograms, palindromes, the 12^12 anticombinatorial sieve) paired with bounded 1d6 tabletop risk |
| Quality and governance loop | One prompter rerolling seeds, or an agent grading its own output | Maker-versus-judge firewall: builders walled off from blind readers and fresh-context critics; failures kept public |
| Human authorial position | Slot-machine operator choosing among thumbnails | Constitutional architect, or a fourth-grade crew whose handwritten words and drawings govern machine execution |

## 2. Taxonomy of the SCION prompt stack: all the ones we use

A "prompt" in agentic cinema is not one paragraph. It is a layered family of compilers and system instructions. Each solves a specific bottleneck in turning human or literary intent into inspectable moving images.

**1. Single-agent classical craft baseline: MULTIPLANE ANIMATION STUDIO** (`elsewhere/studio.html`)
- *What it does.* Turns one coding or drawing model into a classical hand-drawn animation desk governed by 38 numbered laws.
- *Core mechanics.* Temporal difference in place of single-image polish (DRAW, LOOK, COMPARE, ADJUST, DRAW AGAIN); seven depth sheets with inverse-depth parallax; numbered storytelling keys before breakdowns and in-betweens; an internal exposure sheet; a six-pass drawing order; checkpoint rollback when a shot is overworked.
- *Bottleneck exposed.* One agent running every phase hits context exhaustion and correlated self-review blind spots.

**2. Substrate-inversion meta-compilers: SCIONIFIER and SCION_SYSTEM_INSTRUCTION_COMPILER v2.0**
- *What they do.* Compile any craft workflow or domain brief into a standalone SCION system instruction.
- *Core mechanics.* Strip legacy human "cow paths" (the single-viewer 1x timeline, where 100x agent output moves the bottleneck from generating to watching) while keeping the deliberate friction craft, consent and judgment need. Nine mandatory blocks: mission and inputs, intent and authority, domain primitives, quality contract, execution and state, verification and repair, failure and recovery, delivery and stop, start now.

**3. Autonomous swarm production rig: SCION: AUTONOMOUS ANIMATION STUDIO**
- *What it does.* The compilation of the Multiplane Studio through the compiler: hand-drawn and code animation reorganized as a multi-agent swarm under 15 laws.
- *Core mechanics.* Thesis and benchmark bar (laws 1 to 2); representative passage first (law 3); world and performance specificity (laws 5 to 7); quarantined acceptance (law 9, "never let the builder award the pass"); the honesty ledger (laws 11, 14, 15: every claim MEASURED, STRUCTURAL, JUDGED or UNKNOWN), with recursive inquiry when repeated repairs fail.

**4. Constraint-first archival and handmade film crews: PAELEO / SCION / OULIPO** (43 prompts)
- *What they do.* Turn Oulipo and Ouxpo constraints (Oucipo, Oubapo, Outrapo, Oulipopo, Outypopo, Ou'inpo, ALAMO, Oupeinpo, Ouphopo, Oumupo, Ouarchpo, Ougrapo, Ouhispo, Oucuipo, Oupolpot) into executable crew assignments tested on one shared baseline: a visitor, a sealed envelope, a bell, a brass key on a bench, a closed service door.
- *Core mechanics.* The material vow ("foraged or handmade, never faked") across six roles: director, builders, editor, blind readers, independent critic (who fails any cut that breaks Roubaud's law that a constraint must be perceptible), rights reviewer. All failures stay public.

**5. Combinatorial and actuarial video-text rigs: SCION: MVVT** (PAE-03 The Flour Tower, VTX-0.1, PAE-04 The Burning of the Abominable House)
- *What they do.* Operationalize Calvino's 1973 mystery and Braffort's 1977 anticombinatorics as browser-native minimum viable video-text.
- *Core mechanics.* Faceless flour-sack rigs convey weight, hesitation and recoil without bodies; 96 atomic performances, shared transitions, persistent room marks and deterministic agent memory; the 12^12 = 8,916,100,448,256 castings sieved by explicit rules; the 4.6-point payout confound on Widow Roessler's life policy (84.0% to 79.4%).

**6. Participatory classroom and bistable rehydration rigs: PAE-09 / Tourkey Sub 55-porthole SCION matrix**
- *What they do.* Extend SCION from closed machine swarms into a hybrid fourth-grade tabletop and animation loop.
- *Core mechanics.* 55 porthole seeds, each with a clue, a lineage, two bistable readings (Turkey thinks / the trout think) and one rule naming an animation SCION or an Ouxpo constraint. An eight-step, three-seat, pass-left relay (pilot, then Turkey's bolts 1 to 3, then the trout's bolts 4 to 6, then one die) across probable, plausible and preposterous. Completed student sheets then serve as intent contracts that SCION swarms compile into voiced code cartoons.

**7. Cross-domain sibling studios: SCION: HISTORICISING SPECULATIVE DESIGN, SCION LUDIC / GAME STUDIO 2.0, SCION DEMIURGE**
- *What they do.* Generalize the same constitutional architecture to media archaeology, playable game design and navigable 3D worlds.

**8. Upstream extraction and lineage compilers: PATCHWORLD, GOLDING / GOLDEN RIVERBED v12, EYE_LIGHT_GENOME, LINEAGE_GENOME, RIPPLES**
- *What they do.* Supply the analytical inputs SCION swarms consume: reference films parsed into 8 to 10 s patches; standing hinges separated from flow variables; optical and exposure laws; the 18-track Turkey Sub jukebox; nonhuman perspective-locked perturbations.

## 3. Chapter outline: seven sections

**3.1 The paragone of generative cinema: statistical pixel emulsion versus constitutional rigging**
- *Argument.* Places generative animation in the long tension between hand-crafted tweening, procedural rules (Cohen's AARON), kinematic armatures and template cutout software, where tool defaults dictate style. Shows why scale-first training produces visual homogenization and spatial amnesia when applied naively to text-to-video.
- *Spine.* Bajohr (2024), operative ekphrasis; Mitchell (1994), imagetext; Geertz (1973), thick description; Victor (2013); Hartsoe and Bolter, imagetext to environment text to worldtext.
- *Opening hook.* *Nobody Is Holding the Chalk*: unheld scissors cut a rubber boot out of an old poster and drop it in a green gel creek. When a language model makes a film without pixel diffusion, what holds the scissors, what governs the cut, and who owns the story?

**3.2 Text to code to video: drawing an underwater town in deterministic rigs**
- *Argument.* Compiling language into executable drawing code and physics rules solves continuity, object permanence and environmental causality at the root. The model constructs an inspectable world state, not a pixel surface.
- *Case 1.* *TROUTTOWN: Current Events of an underwater town, drawn entirely in code* (1:45): dry-land institutions kept up under river physics, peaking in the roll-call gag ("Finley?" / "Current." / "That isn't an excuse." / "It's literally the current.").
- *Case 2.* MULTIPLANE ANIMATION STUDIO: a scroll-driven seven-plane browser film in which the falling figure passes through the prompt that built her world.

**3.3 Compiling the swarm: from single-agent desk to SCION**
- *Argument.* The step from single-agent prompt craft to multi-agent governance (compiler, then Autonomous Animation Studio): abolish the 1x timeline bottleneck, harden deliberate craft friction and the maker-versus-judge firewall.
- *Mechanics.* The 15 laws, with the upstream compilers.
- *Governance.* Law 9 and the four-label ledger against self-certification (Strathern 1997 on Goodhart).

**3.4 Oulipian anti-chance and the material vow: the Ouxpo constitutions and the flour-sack sieve**
- *Argument.* Formal constraints act as generative engines and diagnostic probes for language-model filmmaking.
- *Evidence A.* The Oulipo Screening Room: the station-incident workshop prompts and the swarm-made proof films under "foraged or handmade, never faked", with failures kept public.
- *Evidence B.* PAE-03 and PAE-04: flour-sack flattening, 96 atomic deeds, the 12^12 sieve, reading over the machine's shoulder.

**3.5 Unheld scissors and archival gel: reflexive materiality in *Nobody Is Holding the Chalk***
- *Argument.* A shot-by-shot reading of the 3:27 film: five tools (01 scissors, 14 scalpel, 35 pins, 44 pen, 03 chalk) working over desks, paper planes and 1940 to 1941 FSA photographs show how the agents forage, cut, layer and inscribe without pretending to have hands.
- *Climax.* Porthole 44 (from 1:55: inked spiral and the palindrome NOW TRAP A POOL · ROTOR · LOOP A PART WON, after *Anémic cinéma*); porthole 03 (from 2:33: HOOK MONSTER, a lipogram shown as washed chalk); the pull-back from 3:10 to ROOM 4 ART CLASS ("We did. We made you a turkey." "Now. What will you make us?").

**3.6 Cinema as a playable tabletop constitution: Tourkey Submersible and the 55-porthole matrix**
- *Argument.* *Tourkey Trouttown* (16:35), with the porthole logs and slips, shows a film operating as a classroom game master and a 55-seed index.
- *Mechanics.* The rules sequence (0:00 to 3:05) and four dives (3:05 to 16:03; porthole 10, Single Hinge, SCION 13, near 5:21) join chance, memory and consequence, LEGOS slots, Oubapo bistable framing, and probable, plausible, preposterous into a pass-left relay with one die.

**3.7 From pencil ring to animated cut: rehydrating fourth-grade worldtexts without sanitization**
- *Argument.* Closes with the classroom run, across *How to Play the Porthole Game* (6:22) and *Tourkey Trouttown: The Class Plays* (14:48). When children's handwritten sheets (portholes 10, 13, 16, 25, 33) serve as binding intent contracts, the swarms preserve child-authored voice, weirdness, blank spaces and margin overflows.
- *Comparison and bridge.* The compact cut against the two-pass film ("First, watch them work", then "Now see what they pictured"). The three permissions ("Blank is okay. Wild is good. Being stuck is okay too.") against sanitization, and the move from 2D apertures to navigable 3D worlds in SCION DEMIURGE.

### Work 1 · TROUTTOWN: Current Events

**Provocation.** Instead of asking a video generator to hallucinate underwater pixels that dissolve at the first cut, this film lets a language model construct an entire submerged Appalachian town in deterministic drawing code, where municipal bureaucracy has to obey river current.

**Case-study analysis.** Opening above a muted creek lined by birch trees and utility poles (0:00, "Up there, this is just a creek"), the camera follows a sheet of notebook paper that lands on the water, radiates ripples (0:06 to 0:08) and sinks (0:09, "Then you go under"). Beneath the surface, planks, tires and eels assemble the word TROUTTOWN (0:15 to 0:17) before the current pulls the letters apart. Across civic tableaux (WELCOME TO TROUTTOWN / POP. 4,812 / ELEV. 0 FT at 0:32; Main Street, a diner, a supermarket aisle where a box of OATS floats off the shelf at 0:43, church pews, a courtroom, elders noting "Current's picking up" at 0:54), trout citizens keep up dry-land institutions while water physics governs every body. The tension peaks in TROUTTOWN ELEMENTARY (1:08 to 1:28): as the teacher calls attendance, Finley drifts off his chair toward the ceiling. "Finley." / "Current." / "That isn't an excuse." / "It's literally the current." Because the model authored geometry, rigs and drift rules in code, every storefront and floating worksheet stays crisp and reproducible to the ending at the playground fence (1:29 to 1:41), where a fishing lure drops from the surface world ("Nobody touch that... Mostly.").
