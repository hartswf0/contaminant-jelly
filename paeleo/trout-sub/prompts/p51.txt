Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass: a dim blue-green grotto, one pale glow. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing enters from the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle; no turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The scene of the porthole reference appears: a giant mossy stone clam on the grotto floor with a cream-white pearl as big as a basketball wedged between its shells, under a rock arch. The same look happens twice: the upper shell lowers and hides the pearl, then lifts; the pearl is glossy and egg-shaped, wobbling. It lowers and lifts again; now the pearl sits tight like a plug and a stream of bubbles leaks around it.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the dented metal tin resting on the grotto floor, the turkey small behind the amber porthole, and in the foreground a speckled brook trout nosing the heavy pearl in the open clam; it rocks like a ball, and the amber beam glints on it like polished brass.

Staging as in the last reference: a vessel's pale beam under a jagged rock arch, kept in full color. Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
