Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. One single continuous 8-second shot, no cuts, camera locked off.

The fixed eye-level view from the pilot's seat inside a small submarine built from a long oval dented riveted steel tin (metal, never bread), exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered round brass six-bolt porthole with hinge, offset latch and a chip in the rim at two o'clock; empty ballast slot at lower left; chalk route chart under glass at lower center; brass lamp toggle at lower right; caged amber lamp overhead; dent in the right wall. The room never changes. The pilot is never seen: only the tip of one brown feathered turkey wing rises from the bottom edge, small, never covering the porthole.

Through the glass, cool blue-green river: a speckled brook trout floats at the right beside a small brass bell hanging from a twig; at the left a pale swirl of silt drifts.

0-3s: the wing tip holds a stub of white chalk and draws one bold white X on the chart under glass, at the end of the chalk route line.
3-6s: the wing tip sets a blank round cream card the size and shape of the porthole on the console beside the chart, and slides it forward toward the viewer until it rests at the very front edge. Outside, the silt swirl thins and vanishes completely.
6-8s: the trout outside nudges the bell with its nose; the bell swings and rings, bright, twice. The wing tip withdraws. The X and the round card stay.

From the last reference take only the idea of a fish close beside a small hanging brass bell; not its photographic look, glasses, tie or other fish. Bubbles rise. No text, no labels, no captions.
