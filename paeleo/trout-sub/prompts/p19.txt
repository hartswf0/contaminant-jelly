Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower left corner and drops a small grey iron weight into the ballast slot. The tin sinks: the water in the porthole slides upward in wide rounded bands, each band darker blue-green, layered as in the last reference.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): the camera pushes slowly in to the glass as the sinking stops at the scene of the porthole reference: a raw brown potato standing on the riverbed, skewered with ten striped bendy drinking straws sticking out all round like spines, lit amber from below. As the water darkens the ten straws all bend at their elbows together and swing to point the same way, up and to the right, then hold still, quivering; one bead of air leaves each straw tip. The potato creeps one small step sideways on its lower straws. A faint reflection of the turkey's face sits low left on the glass.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin settled on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the straw-spiked potato and one speckled brook trout that swims a slow circle around it, peering at each straw, then stops with its open mouth almost touching the potato skin, where one small bite is already missing.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
