Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted metal bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass two pale spotted tree trunks stand in the river. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing enters from the lower left edge and drops a small grey ballast weight into the slot.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): slow push in to the glass onto the scene of the porthole reference: two thin old men in waistcoats, flat jointed puppets with brass pins at shoulders, elbows and knees, stand on a mossy bank between the twin trees, a carved boundary stone at lower right. Staged as in the last two references: the trees and ground strip never move, only the figures change. Together each swings one stiff arm up from the shoulder pin like a toll gate, then both turn sideways and become paper-thin edges. A faint turkey face reflects low on the glass.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the dented steel tin now resting on the bank right beside the boundary stone, its amber porthole glow on the stone, the turkey small behind the glass. The twin trunks stand unmoved behind. One elder points a pinned arm at the stone, the other holds out a coin as if to board. A speckled brook trout in the foreground looks from stone to tin.
