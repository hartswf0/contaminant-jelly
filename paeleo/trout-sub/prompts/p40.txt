Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, dim blue-green water and pale stacked towers. Only the tip of one feathered turkey wing reaches in from the lower right edge and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on and the camera pushes in to the glass. The scene of the porthole reference: a reef of white sugar-cube towers, two grey reel-to-reel tape reels lying on the pebbles, and long loose ribbons of brown audio tape waving upward like kelp. In the lamp the nearest ribbon glints cranberry red. Then everything runs backward: the reels spin in reverse and the ribbons are drawn down and wound back onto them, one loop sliding toward the glass as it goes. A speckled brook trout holds still among the towers. Pale towers recede into the murk as in the last reference.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the dented metal tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole, the tin rocking slightly and its lamp pulsing. In the foreground the speckled trout beside a spinning reel watches one tape ribbon pulled taut between the reel and the tin's hull, bubbles rising backward, downward.
