Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass is dim blue-green murk and one dark curved arch. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on below the porthole and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The murk clears to the scene of the porthole reference: a mossy boulder arch, a small brass bell hung from a twig at the top of the arch, a thin knotted cord dangling from its clapper. A speckled brook trout swims one slow clockwise circle around the bell, then its tail brushes the cord. The bell swings and rings, and the trout stops dead with its mouth open. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass tilts.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin resting on the pebble riverbed under the mossy arch, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. The bell hangs above the tin and the knot of its cord is snagged on the tin's top handle; the tin rocks and the bell rings again. In the foreground, close to camera above the stony bottom as in the last reference, the trout finishes its clockwise circle and turns to stare in at the turkey.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
