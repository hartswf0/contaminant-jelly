Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass is dim blue-green murk crossed by faint pale straight lines. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on below the porthole and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The murk clears to the scene of the porthole reference: a wall of thick wavy cyan bathroom glass blocks, three rows of three, pale grout lines between them, filling the porthole. Behind the wall one speckled brook trout swims left to right, cut into squares by the blocks. The middle row of blocks shows its piece of the trout slipped one block sideways, out of step with its head and tail, then all nine pieces snap back into one whole trout. Water ripples inside the glass as in the last reference: wide soft ribbons of blue. A faint reflection of the turkey's face, low left on the glass, is split by the grout lines too.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, seen through the same wall of nine cyan glass blocks standing in front of it. Each of the nine blocks holds its own tiny amber-lit turkey behind a tiny porthole, nine small turkeys in a grid. The amber light flickers and the middle block goes dark, then lights again. In the foreground one speckled trout hangs before the wall and tilts its head at it.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
