Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, dark blue-green water and one faint pale band sliding past. Only the tip of one turkey wing enters at the lower right corner and flips the brass toggle. No turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: a mossy stone arch tunnel in dark teal water, and hundreds of tiny silver minnows streaming as one single ribbon in a big S-curve, every minnow following the one ahead, none leaving the band. The front of the ribbon is a sharp arrowhead of minnows that points up to the right, into the arch. The tail of the ribbon curls into a round loop around a riverbed stone and draws tight. The minnows flash silver in the beam. A faint turkey reflection low left on the glass looks from the loop to the arrowhead. The ribbon moves as flat wide bands, as in the last reference.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the long oval riveted metal tin with its handle on the pebble riverbed before the mossy arch, the turkey small inside the round amber porthole. The same silver ribbon of minnows swings in one S-curve and falls into line behind the tin, so the tin sits at the head of the ribbon like the first float of a parade, glinting the same silver as the minnows. One speckled brook trout in the left foreground looks from the tin to the minnows and back.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
