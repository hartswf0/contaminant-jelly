Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass is dim blue-green murk and one boxy dark shape far off. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower left corner and drops a small iron ballast weight into the slot with a clunk.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The murk clears to the scene of the porthole reference: a drowned cracked road with faded yellow dashes on the riverbed, and a speckled brook trout gripping the handle of a rusty wire shopping cart loaded with dented tin cans, rolling straight at the porthole. The creek runs the wrong way: silt plumes, weed and bubbles all stream toward the glass, and the current drags the cart forward nose first while the trout hangs on behind and is towed, tail trailing, cans rattling. The cart grows large in the glass. A faint reflection of the turkey's face at the lower right of the glass leans back.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin lying across the cracked road on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the cart stops with its wire nose an inch from the tin. The trout frowns at the tin blocking the way, then taps the steel with one fin so it rings hollow, looks at the cans in the cart, and looks back at the tin. Water in soft layered ribbons with small round bubbles as in the last reference, all sliding the same way.
