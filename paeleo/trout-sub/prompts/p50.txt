Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Pressed close against the glass, one huge dark banded fish flank fills the whole port like a wall. Only the tip of one feathered wing enters at the lower right edge and flips the brass toggle; no turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on under the port and the camera pushes in to the glass. The fish drifts back from the glass and shrinks to what the porthole reference shows: a three-inch tan snail darter with dark saddle bands, hovering in front of a big rusty orange diamond road sign that stands in the river gravel and reads DAM. The darter only fans its fins and holds its place. Behind the sign's edges four large speckled trout arrive, stop short and wait in a line, none passing the tiny fish. The turkey's faint reflection low left on the glass tilts its head.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside reference: the steel tin with its amber porthole on the riverbed, the turkey small inside. Scale reversed: the darter is now nearest the camera and looms larger than the tin, head down, shoving a low ridge of pebbles forward with its snout like a blade; behind it a fresh trench runs to the foot of the small far DAM sign, silt clouding up from it. A speckled trout at the left edge stares. Gravel bed as in the last reference: rounded pebbles over fine grit, near view.

Bubbles rise throughout. The only lettering is the word DAM on the sign; no other text, no labels, no captions.
