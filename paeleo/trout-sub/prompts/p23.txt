Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass the river is dim dusk blue with one pale round shape. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing enters at the lower right edge and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The beam finds the scene of the porthole reference: a round cream wall clock with a dark rim on a mossy barnacled wooden post, ringed by a pink sea anemone whose long tentacles are the dial. With each tick the tentacles curl inward over the clock face like a closing mouth, then open; the minute hand jumps from twelve to quarter past three. A small speckled trout hovers at the three like an hour hand. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass tilts.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the steel tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the anemone clock swings gently from a short brass chain held in the fin of a large old trout in round spectacles, like a pocketwatch, while a line of small trout fry with satchels hurries past late. Dusk water in layered rounded blue bands as in the last reference.

Bubbles rise throughout. Ticking sound. The only lettering is the clock numerals 1 to 12; no other text, no labels, no captions.
