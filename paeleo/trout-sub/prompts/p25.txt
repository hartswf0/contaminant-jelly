Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass is a dark blue-green rock cave with one small upright shape. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing enters at the lower right edge and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The beam finds the scene of the porthole reference: a speckled brook trout walking upright on two legs along a rock ledge between stalactites, wearing a brass riveted collar with a ribbed hose and, over its head, a round glass fishbowl with its open mouth turned upward, full of water. The helmet is inverted: the water is inside the bowl. As the trout steps, the water in the bowl sloshes and small bubbles rise only inside the bowl; around the trout nothing drifts, no bubbles, no silt. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass at lower left tilts.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the steel tin with its top handle resting on the cave floor, looming like the helmet of a giant diving suit, the turkey small behind the amber porthole as if behind a visor. In the foreground the fishbowl trout, seen from behind at the left, stops and leans toward the port; the round porthole and the tiny turkey are mirrored on the curve of its bowl, like a bird in a bottle. The brass rim is a hard threshold between wet and dry, as in the last reference.
