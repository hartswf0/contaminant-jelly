Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 3 (7-10s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the riverbed, lit amber like a covered roasting dish and like a landed saucer, the turkey small behind the porthole turning a little valve with one wing tip.
