Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. One single continuous 8-second shot, no cuts, camera locked off.

The fixed eye-level view from the pilot's seat inside a small submarine built from a long oval dented riveted steel tin (metal, never bread), exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered round brass six-bolt porthole with hinge, offset latch and a chip in the rim at two o'clock; empty ballast slot at lower left; chalk route chart under glass at lower center; brass lamp toggle at lower right; caged amber lamp overhead; dent in the right wall. The room never changes. The pilot is never seen: only the tip of one brown feathered turkey wing rises from the bottom right edge, small, never covering the porthole.

Through the glass is a river bend: grey stone steps climbing the bank at the left, two dark upright tree trunks at the right, and between them a far rock with a V-shaped notch. These never move.

0-2.5s: a brown cloud of silt rolls across the bend and buries the rock; the notch is gone, the steps and both trunks still show at the edges.
2.5-4s: the wing tip flips the brass toggle.
4-7s: a narrow amber beam shines out from below the porthole, sweeps slowly from the steps toward the trunks and stops on the rock; inside the beam the silt glows thin and the V notch appears again, sharp and amber-lit, in exactly the same place.
7-8s: the beam holds on the notch; the wing tip rests on the chart.

From the last reference take only how a pale cone of light cuts through a grainy field; not its grey color, black bars or vessels. Bubbles drift past. No text, no labels, no captions.
