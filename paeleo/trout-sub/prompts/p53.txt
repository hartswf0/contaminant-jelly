The wheel turns forward, each paddle pressing a neat row of notches into the silt like a crimped pie edge, then a paddle catches the brass latch with a spark and a click: the wheel jams, rolls backward one paddle, and the trapdoor lifts a hand's width and drops shut. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass tilts, puzzled.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. The wheel rocks forward, back, forward against the log. Water as in the last reference: wide flat layered ribbons of blue current.
