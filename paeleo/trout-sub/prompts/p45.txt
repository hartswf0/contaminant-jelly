Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Only the tip of one feathered turkey wing enters from the lower left edge and drops a small iron ballast weight into the slot with a clunk. The view outside the glass sinks slightly.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, onto the scene of the porthole reference: a coral-red plastic funnel hanging on two thin strings above the riverbed. A red-bulbed kitchen baster is drawn tip-first into its wide mouth and shrinks away, a made thing being un-made, while small round coloured pellets, red, yellow, teal and cream, pop one by one out of the narrow brass spout. Then the funnel swings on its strings until its wide mouth faces the porthole. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass leans back.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole, and in the foreground the hanging funnel with a speckled brook trout beside it. The pellets drift upward like soap bubbles and the trout noses one, while the funnel's wide mouth lifts grains of gravel off the riverbed in a thin stream. Water and drifting round shapes move in soft flat layers as in the last reference.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
