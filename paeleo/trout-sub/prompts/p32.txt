Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass only blue-green murk and a folded dark zigzag shape at the left edge. Only the tip of one turkey wing enters at the lower right corner and flips the brass toggle. No turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on below the port and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference. From a slot in the yellow hull at the left a brass scissor-jack arm unfolds, its crossed bars stretching out link by link, the only thing that moves. At its end a brass two-jaw claw pinches an old iron skeleton key. The claw snaps open and shut once like a crab's pincer without dropping the key, then twists the key a quarter turn in the empty water as if in a keyhole. A speckled brook trout below gapes up at it. A faint turkey reflection low left on the glass tilts its head.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. The same scissor arm reaches out of the tin's left side into the foreground. A trout holds its mouth wide open and the claw brings the key tip to its teeth, close and near the lens like the fish and brass object in the last reference. Behind it a second trout hides a small padlocked tackle box under its fin.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
