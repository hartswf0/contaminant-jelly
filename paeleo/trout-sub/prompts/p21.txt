Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass only murky blue-green water and five small cyan glows in a row. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on below the porthole and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, onto the scene of the porthole reference: a sunken yellow bus on the rocky riverbed, and in its five windows five trout heads far too big for the windows, like cut-out paper pasted at the wrong scale, each tapping a glowing cyan tablet. All five tap at the same instant and a pale ring spreads from each tablet toward the glass; then the heads sway together as if the bus hit a bump. Warm wash on the heads and cyan screen glow as in the last reference.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the same yellow bus is now toy-sized, no bigger than the speckled brook trout beside it, a brass wind-up key turning on its roof; its wheels spin in the silt and it does not move. The trout waits by a bare wooden post, tapping one fin, looking down the river.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
