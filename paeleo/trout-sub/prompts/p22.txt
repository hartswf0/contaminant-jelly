Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with hinge, latch and a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, in murky blue-green water, a row of three dim upright shapes bobs down and up together. Only the tip of one feathered turkey wing reaches in from the lower left edge and drops a small round iron ballast weight with a ring handle into the ballast slot. No turkey body, no tail fan.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The water clears to the scene of the porthole reference: three speckled trout standing upright in a tight row in mid-water, fins held straight out in front, facing a larger trout coach with a whistle on a red cord around its neck. The coach gives one sharp nod and the three squat down and rise in exact unison, twice, the same move repeated identically like a loop; three columns of bubbles rise in step. Shoulder to shoulder they bar the whole width of the port. A faint reflection of the turkey's face low on the glass bobs with them.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin with its top handle resting on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole tapping one wing tip in steady time. In the foreground, seen from behind, the coach and the three trout keep squatting in the same unison loop beside the tin, which sits among them like a big weight with a handle.

Soft layered bands of blue water as in the last reference; take only that light. Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
