Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass is dim blue-green murk and one squat barrel-shaped dark form. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle with a click.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on below the porthole and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The murk clears to the scene of the porthole reference: a plain orange plastic picnic water cooler jug with a white lid and a white spigot, tipped over on mossy river stones like dropped litter, trailing long loops of clear vinyl tubing. Two lengths of tube are spliced together in the middle with a lumpy wrap of twine and a patch. Bubbles pump through the tube in a slow steady rhythm, in and out like breathing, swelling at the splice and streaming up from the far end beside the gills of a speckled brook trout who hovers at the right. A faint reflection of the turkey's face at the lower left of the glass tilts.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the orange cooler on its stones, and its spliced clear tube now runs across to the tin with its loose end stuck against the steel hull like a drinking straw. The trout, near the camera above the stony bottom as in the last reference, noses the white spigot; a short fizzy jet of bubbles squirts out. The trout looks along the tube to the tin, then back at the spigot.

Bubbles rise throughout. The cooler is blank. No text, no labels, no captions.
