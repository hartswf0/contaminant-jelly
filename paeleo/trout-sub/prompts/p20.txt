Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the references.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower right corner and flips the lamp toggle. Outside the glass one white sunbeam slants down through dim blue-green water.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, onto the scene of the porthole reference: a tall pointed lancet window standing on the riverbed, its arch built of coloured river pebbles, its panes stained glass with a flower rosette at the top. The sunbeam passes through the panes and splits into red, gold, green and violet shafts that sweep across the riverbed in broken patches, like the patch of light on the floor in the last reference. Six speckled trout hang in two facing rows before the window, change colour as the shafts cross them, and open their mouths together. A faint reflection of the turkey's face sits low left on the glass.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the pebble arch at the left edge. The coloured shafts sweep over the tin; the brass porthole rim flares gold like the bell of a horn and one fat bubble leaves it. Behind the glass the turkey, small, lit amber, spreads both wings, a ring of light behind its head. One trout in the foreground stares in.
