Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. A torn cream paper creek map is stuck flat across the inside of the porthole glass, blue-green river showing at its ragged edges. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle; the amber lamp brightens.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: the map has wavy inked creek lines and one bold black X, and a giant brown garden snail with a golden spiral shell crawls on the paper, on our side of the glass. It creeps in a slow curve around the X, leaving a shiny wet ring, then slides its big shell right over the X so the X is completely hidden. Its mouth works at the paper. A speckled brook trout watches from the water at upper right; a faint reflection of the turkey's face at lower left tilts, puzzled. Warm sepia paper grain as in the last reference.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed. The amber porthole is covered from inside by the pale glowing back of the map, the turkey small, peeking past its torn edge. Pressed against the glass, lit from behind, the round spiral shell looks like one huge golden eye with a dark spiral pupil. It slides slowly sideways like an eye turning, then stops, still and smooth as a round pebble. In the foreground a speckled brook trout stares at it.
