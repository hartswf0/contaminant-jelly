Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, dim blue-green water and one dark shape with a small unlit round window. Only one turkey wing tip enters at the lower right corner and flips the brass toggle. No turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on below the porthole and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: a second small steel tin submarine with copper pipes and its own round brass porthole. One second late, its window lights amber too, and a tiny turkey inside raises a wing and waves. A faint turkey reflection low left on the glass waves first; the tiny turkey copies it a beat later, like an echo. A speckled trout hovers beside it.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. The twin tin now sits nose to nose against it, overlapping, so they read as one long steel body with two glowing amber eyes; both turkeys wave, the second a beat late. Three trout queue behind, blocked; the front one backs away. Deep cyan water with small points of lamp light as in the last reference: only its light, none of its craft or figures.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
