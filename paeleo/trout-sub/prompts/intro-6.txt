Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. One single continuous 8-second shot, no cuts: a wide establishing view of a small town on the bed of a cool blue-green river, camera drifting very slowly forward.

The town, held still for the whole shot: in the left foreground a low wooden counter with one amber shaded work lamp glowing warm and a row of small plain coloured squares, and behind it an upright speckled brook trout in an apron wiping the counter. In the middle distance two tall bare tree trunks stand like a gate at the bend of the river. To the right of the trunks shallow stone steps climb along the bank, and an upright trout in a work vest walks down them. At the foot of the steps a small brass bell hangs on a post with a third upright trout waiting beside it. Pale rock with one notch far behind. Slanting sun shafts, drifting silt, rising bubbles.

0-3s: the residents work; far off between the two trunks a small amber glow appears in the murk.
3-6s: the glow becomes the submarine of the outside reference: a long oval dented riveted steel tin (metal, never bread) with a handle on top, a dent on its right side and a centered round brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, a small turkey behind the amber glass. It glides in between the trunks and its amber beam sweeps over the stone steps.
6-8s: the tin settles on the pebbles by the counter. All three trout turn to look at it; the trout at the post rings the brass bell.

From the last two references take only staging and light: upright residents at trout height among trunks, sun cutting down through water. Do not copy their figures, clothes, tools or photographic look. No text, no labels, no captions.
