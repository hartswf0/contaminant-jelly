Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, in blue-green murk, a faint green glow low down. Only the tip of one turkey wing enters at the lower right corner and flips the brass toggle. No turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: a little theatre on the riverbed made of flat painted cardboard cutouts, scalloped cream curtains, plank stage floor, and in the floor a round hole glowing bright green. The push-in passes the flat layers one by one like ribs, each curtain layer sliding sideways in parallax, edges of cut card showing. A leaf and a thread of silt circle the green hole and are pulled down into it. A speckled brook trout watches from the right wing. A faint turkey reflection low left on the glass tilts its head.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground, side on, the flat cardboard stage, thin as card. The green hole throws a cone of green light straight up like a spotlight, hard-edged as the cut shapes in the last reference. One trout drops a pebble into a tin cup and swims into the green cone, lit; two more trout hang in a row staring past it at the turkey in the port.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
