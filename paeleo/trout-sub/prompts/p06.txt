Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous 8-second film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Only the tip of one feathered wing reaches in at lower right and flips the lamp toggle. An amber beam switches on below the porthole.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): slow push in to the glass. The beam finds the scene of the porthole reference: a big olive carp with a drooping moustache, a stern frown and a tin star badge, holding a yellow wooden ruler upright beside a small bright fishing lure with orange and teal feathers and a hook, by a wooden post and reeds. He measures the lure's feather tail, shakes his head, then swings the ruler round and lays it flat against the glass, measuring the faint reflected turkey face at lower left instead.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole, one speckled brook trout watching in the left foreground. The carp holds the feathered lure up beside the port so its feathers sit next to the turkey's tail fan, looks from one to the other, then raises a fin in salute. Light as in the last reference: one thin pale cone cutting through dim grainy water, here the amber beam.

Bubbles rise throughout. The only lettering is the word SHERIFF on the star badge; no other text, no numbers, no captions.
