Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, blue-green water curling in a slow eddy around one small dark tube. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing reaches in from the lower right and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: a corked brass-capped glass tube stuffed with rolled pastel postcards. The eddy carries it once round in a full circle, back to where it began. As it passes close, one card faces the glass: wavy ink scribble lines and a tiny drawing of a round brass porthole with a tiny tube inside it. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass leans in, then pulls back.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the metal tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the same corked tube stands upright in the eddy and a speckled brook trout pokes a small leaf at the cork like posting a letter; the tube glints green in the amber beam, the trout darts back, and the tube begins the same circle again. Water as in the last reference: wide flat layered ribbons of blue current.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
