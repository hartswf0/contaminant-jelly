Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, murk and a dark shape of upright tubes. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing reaches in from the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on and the camera pushes in to the glass. The murk clears to the scene of the porthole reference: a brass boiler with gears and a tall rank of copper organ pipes over pleated bellows. Its front wall is cut away like a painted cross-section, flat and side-on as in the last reference: inside, an orange firebox glows and three tiny trout in a row pump the bellows. The bellows squeeze and the pipes puff bubbles. The turkey's faint reflection on the glass leans closer.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the same cutaway boiler organ sits on a low flat raft; its pleated bellows look like radiator fins and shake, the pipes toot bubbles in rhythm, and one speckled brook trout beside it bobs in time, looking in at the turkey.

Take only the flat cutaway staging and amber tint from the last reference; no people. Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
