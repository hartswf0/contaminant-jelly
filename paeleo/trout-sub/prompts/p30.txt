Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, dark blue-green murk with six dim round shapes hanging in a row. Only one turkey wing tip enters at the lower right and flips the toggle. No turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: a mossy stone bridge arch over the riverbed and, hanging in a level row under it, six large golden glass bubbles, each holding one capital letter: A, E, I, O, U, Y. They bob like moored floats. Then they light one at a time from left to right, glowing like the pale circles in the last reference, but the second bubble, E, stays dark every time. A speckled trout waits at the right.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. The row of letter bubbles floats in the foreground between the trout and the tin. The turkey pecks the inside of the glass once; at that instant the dark E bubble bursts in one bright flash. The five others glow on. The trout jerks back and stares at the turkey's beak.

The only lettering is the six single letters A E I O U Y inside the bubbles; no other text, no labels, no captions.
