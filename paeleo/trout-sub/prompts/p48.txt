Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Only one feathered wing tip reaches in from the lower left edge and drops a small iron ballast weight into the slot. The tin rises: the green water in the port slides down until the waterline cuts the glass in half.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): slow push in to the glass, showing the porthole reference: a flooded white tin church steeple at the waterline. Above the water, in dry bright air, its pointed corrugated roof and a plain iron arrow weathervane on a thin rod; wind hiss, the arrow swings a quarter turn. Below the water, in green hush, the open belfry arch with a dark bell hanging still. The turkey's faint reflection on the glass looks up at the roof.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the metal tin floating half-submerged, the turkey small behind the amber porthole, and in the foreground, underwater, a speckled brook trout beside the belfry arch. The bell swings once and its clapper strikes, a muffled underwater clang with rings spreading in the water, while above the surface the arrow turns silently. The trout looks from bell to arrow. Water drawn in flat layered bands as in the last reference.
