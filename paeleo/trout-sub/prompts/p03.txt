Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-3s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, dim blue-green water and a dark rectangle. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing enters at the lower right edge and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (3-7s): an amber beam comes on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The rectangle is the porthole reference: a wood-framed green chalkboard standing on the riverbed among weeds, chalked HOOK MONSTER beside a chalk sketch of a beaked, feathered bird hanging on a fish hook. Above the words one more chalk line is already a pale washed smear; a ribbon of current slides over the board and carries off chalk dust in a milky trail, so the heading can never be read. The chalk glows pale against the dark board, as the chalk-like marks do in the last reference. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass looks from the sketch to itself.

Beat 3 (7-10s), hard cut across the glass to the outside reference: the tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground, low in frame as the pupils are in the fourth reference, two young trout with satchels by the chalkboard: one swims up and holds out a worm to the glass, the other ducks behind the board frame, pointing a fin from the sketch to the turkey.

Bubbles rise throughout. The only lettering is HOOK MONSTER on the chalkboard; no other text, no labels, no captions.
