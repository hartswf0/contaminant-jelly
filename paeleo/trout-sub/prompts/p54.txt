Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, murky blue-green water and a dim rock ledge with one still ruffled shape under it. Only the tip of a feathered wing enters at the lower right and flips the brass toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on below the porthole and the camera pushes in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference. Under the mossy slab lies a ruffled brown gravy boat: curled tail as the handle, blunt end as the spout, a frilly rim along its side. Then the same body is read again: a small eye opens on the spout, a webbed foot spreads on the gravel, and it is a wrinkled giant salamander whose frilly side folds sway in slow wide ribbons, as the layered water ribbons of the last reference. A speckled trout watches at the left; the turkey's faint reflection on the glass tilts.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside reference: the steel tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber port. In front, the salamander and the trout face the tin. The salamander's throat folds wobble and the turkey's red wattle wobbles in the same rhythm; then the turkey ducks down inside the tin and peeks back out over the rim.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
