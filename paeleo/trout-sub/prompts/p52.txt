Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass: murky blue-green water and one dark red diagonal line. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing enters from the lower left corner and drops a small iron ballast weight into the slot; no turkey body. The cabin jolts.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The scene of the porthole reference appears: a thick twisted red rope running taut from the lower left up to a knot on an iron ring in a mossy stone wall, its far end lost in a murky pile of boulders. The rope does one thing: it slackens and curls in a slow soft S like a living arm, then snaps straight and hard, grinding a puff of sand off the rocks. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass flinches.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the dented metal tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. The same red rope is now seen from the river: tied to the tin's top handle, stretched tight across a small arched doorway in the stone wall. A speckled brook trout waits in the doorway behind the rope as the rope drags one boulder clear of the doorway.

Light and rope fibre as in the last reference: coarse strands, soft blue depth; nothing else from it. Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
