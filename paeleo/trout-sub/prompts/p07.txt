Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass is dim blue-green murk and one dark spiked ball hanging on a line, like a mine. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on below the porthole and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The beam shows the scene of the porthole reference: the spiked ball is a star-shaped paper pinata in striped fringe of rust, green, blue and violet with ribbon tails, tied by a knotted rope to a mossy stone. Eight speckled brook trout hang in a ring around it, facing it, mouths open, humming one long note. The note slides upward in pitch, and as it climbs the ring of trout rises and tightens toward the pinata and the pinata lifts until its rope pulls taut. A faint reflection of the turkey's face at the lower left of the glass tilts, puzzled.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the pinata sways on its rope and one trout holds a short plain brass tube in its mouth. It blows a buzzing stream of bubbles through the tube as the note slides back down, then swings the tube up toward the pinata and stops just short. Water in soft layered ribbons with small round bubbles as in the last reference.
