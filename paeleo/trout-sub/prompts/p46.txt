Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot at lower left, chalk route chart under glass at lower center, brass lamp toggle at lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Only the tip of one feathered turkey wing reaches in from the lower left edge and drops a small grey lead weight into the ballast slot with a clunk. The room sinks slightly; bubbles rise past the glass.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): slow push in to the porthole glass. Outside, as in the porthole reference, six big solid brass block letters stand on a mossy underwater slope as a staircase climbing left to right, one letter per step, spelling G, O, B, B, L, E. Speckled trout drift beside the steps. The top letter E wobbles, tips off its step and drops slowly, heavy as lead, trailing bubbles, while the lower letters stay put like a ladder. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass looks up the steps.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole, and in the foreground the brass letter staircase seen from the river side. One speckled brook trout swims up the letters step by step like free stairs; a second trout darts back as the loose E lands in the silt with a puff.

Stair diagonal against a round window as in the stair reference; brass against river stones as in the last reference. The only lettering is the six brass letters G O B B L E; no other text, no labels, no captions.
