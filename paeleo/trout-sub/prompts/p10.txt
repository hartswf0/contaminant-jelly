Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, in blue-green murk, a mossy red brick wall with a big dark clam shape fixed to it. Only the tip of one turkey wing enters at the lower right corner and flips the brass toggle. No turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: a giant pale ribbed clam shell bolted to the sunken schoolhouse wall by one brass hinge pin at its back. Nothing else on it moves: the top half swings up on that pin and claps shut, three times. Each clap sends a ring of ripples across the water and three speckled brook trout jump back, then drift close again. A faint turkey reflection low left on the glass flinches.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the same clam on its brick wall. Trout swim past beneath it in a straight single file. The last trout stops, lifts the top shell with its nose to peek inside, and the lid drops shut on its hinge, standing and falling like the lid in the last reference.
