Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass is dim blue-green murk and one boxy dark shape. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on below the porthole and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The murk clears to the scene of the porthole reference: an old domed wooden steamer trunk on the riverbed, brass bands, leather straps, barnacles and moss, with one star-shaped hole cut in its front. The star glows from inside. A trout's eye comes to the star hole and peers out at the viewer, blinks, then the lid lifts a crack and one gold glint and the end of a red silk scarf drift out. A faint reflection of the turkey's face sits low left on the glass. Cut-out light as in the last reference: light leaking through star shapes cut in a dark hinged lid.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole with one eye pressed to the glass. In the foreground, seen from behind, the trunk with its lid now open and a blank paper luggage tag swaying on its handle; a speckled trout rises out of the trunk with the red scarf in its mouth and bows toward the tin, and the star hole throws a star of light on the pebbles.
