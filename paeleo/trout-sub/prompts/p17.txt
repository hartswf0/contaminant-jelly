Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass is dim blue-green murk with a low round shape. Only the tip of one turkey wing reaches in at the lower right corner and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on below the porthole and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. The murk clears to the scene of the porthole reference: five stern speckled trout in round spectacles seated around a mossy round stone table set with small blank slate tablets and stacks of pebbles. Four of them rap a fin on the stone in the same slow rhythm, all together, again and again, getting nowhere. The middle trout alone breaks step: it stops, turns and stares straight out at the glass, and the amber beam lights its spectacles. A faint reflection of the turkey's face sits low left on the glass. Seating as in the last reference: fish on different sides of a pale low table in dim water.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole, one wing raised and held still. In the foreground the stone table and the backs of the five trout, all now facing the tin. The middle trout swims up to the tin and sets one pebble from its stack on the riverbed under the porthole; the amber lamp in the tin blinks once.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
