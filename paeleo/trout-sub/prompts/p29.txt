Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, blue-green murk; one small pale square of paper drifts slowly past. Only the tip of one turkey wing enters at the lower right corner and flips the brass toggle. No turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: a thick old photo album lying open on the mossy riverbed, its cover flap slack like the mouth of a sack. The album never moves. The current lifts one page and sepia snapshots of little cabins and a covered bridge slip out one after another and drift up to the right in a single slow line, like letters spilling from a bag, on wide soft ribbons of water as in the last reference. A speckled trout at the right watches them pass. A faint turkey reflection low left on the glass.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin with its handle on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small and perfectly still inside the round brass porthole like a portrait in a round frame. The same line of sepia snapshots drifts across from the open album at the left. The porthole lamp gives one white flash like a flashbulb; one snapshot settles against the brass rim. The speckled trout in the foreground flinches, then stares at the porthole.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
