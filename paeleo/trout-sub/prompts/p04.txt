Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-3s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, dim blue-green water and one low dark dome under a faint stone arch. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing enters at the lower right edge and flips the brass lamp toggle.

Beat 2 (3-7s): an amber beam comes on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass. It shows the porthole reference: a mossy old stone bridge arch, and under it a big moss-backed snapping turtle with one clawed foot on a large brass key lying on the riverbed. As the beam touches it, the turtle pulls head, legs and tail inside its shell and goes still: now only a smooth mossy dome sits under the arch, a single brass glint at its lower edge and a thin string of bubbles rising from its rim. The pale beam and the dome under the arch sit as the hull and beam do in the last reference. A faint reflection of the turkey's face on the glass tilts, unsure.

Beat 3 (7-10s), hard cut across the glass to the outside reference: the tin on the riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the shell under the bridge arch, and a speckled brook trout hovering beside it, watching. The turtle's head and one foreleg slide out just past the shell rim; the claw hooks the ring of the brass key, drags it close, and rubs the key's teeth slowly along the mossy edge of its shell.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
