Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted metal bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass: dim blue-green river. Only the tip of a feathered turkey wing enters at the lower right edge and flips the lamp toggle; an amber beam switches on outside.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): slow push in to the glass. Into the beam glides the animal of the porthole reference: a plump yellow shark with brown crayon leopard spots and a cream belly, a small speckled trout trailing it. It presses its blunt cream snout flat against the glass. Its mouth opens, showing small teeth, then closes into a smile and it nudges the glass twice. The turkey's faint reflection sits low left on the glass.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the dented steel tin on river pebbles, drawn as a dollhouse cutaway: left of the porthole a strip of steel wall is sliced away, showing a flat side-on section of the amber interior with a shelf of small round cans, staged like the cut-open hull of the last reference (only that cross-section staging; no people, no signs). The turkey is small behind the amber porthole, head and chest only. The glow turns the oval tin yellow, the same shape as the yellow shark floating nose to the port. The shark nudges; the tin rocks and one can drops onto the pebbles. The trout in the foreground watches.

Bubbles rise throughout. No text, no labels, no captions.
