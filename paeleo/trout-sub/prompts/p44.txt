Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart under glass, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, in blue-green murk, one big round dark disc on a stand. Only the tip of one turkey wing enters at the lower right corner and flips the brass toggle. No turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: a large flat disc painted with one black-and-white spiral, bolted at its hub to a rusty iron A-frame stand on the riverbed. Only the disc turns: it spins steadily about its hub, and as it turns the flat spiral seems to sink into a deep tunnel and then swell out toward the glass. Bubbles and weed tips bend and curl toward its center in wide flat ribbons of water like the last reference. A speckled brook trout at the right drifts slowly toward the hub, staring. A faint turkey reflection low left on the glass sways with the spiral.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground, seen from the side, the same spiral disc on its A-frame. A trout flicks the rim with its tail; the disc slows, ticking, and stops while a short line of trout waits beside it. Soap bubbles froth from the hub and one striped sock tumbles round with the disc.
