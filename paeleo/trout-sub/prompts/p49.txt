Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. In the murky port two small blurred glows, one red above one green, hang in the dark. Only the tip of one feathered wing enters at the lower right edge and flips the brass toggle; no turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam comes on under the port and the camera pushes in to the glass. The murk clears to the porthole reference: a yellow four-sided traffic signal on a sagging cable over a drowned street of wooden storefronts, red and green lamps both lit like holiday bulbs. A speckled trout in a blue police cap and badge blows a whistle; the signal swings sideways off its plumb line, the green lamp goes dark and only the red stays, round and hard. The turkey's faint reflection low on the glass tilts its head. Drowned street light as in the last reference: pale uprights fading into blue-green.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside reference: the steel tin with its amber porthole, the turkey small inside, drifts along the street bed under the swinging signal, which is still red, its yellow beam on the road ahead like a headlamp. In the foreground the trout in the police cap raises one fin flat to halt it while a small minnow beside him waves a fin to hail it.
