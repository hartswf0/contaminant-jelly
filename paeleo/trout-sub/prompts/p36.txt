Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Three beats, one continuous film.

Beat 1 (0-2.5s), Turkey's seat: the fixed eye-level view inside the riveted steel bread-roll-tin submarine exactly as in the cockpit reference: centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk route chart under glass lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. Outside the glass, in blue-green murk, the dark shape of a sitting dog in front of a sunken bench. Only the tip of one turkey wing enters at the lower right corner and flips the brass toggle. No turkey body.

Beat 2 (2.5-5.5s): an amber beam switches on and the camera pushes slowly in to the glass, to the scene of the porthole reference: a brown hound sitting upright on the riverbed before a sunken wooden park bench, a white envelope with a red wax seal held in its mouth. The hound stays in exactly the same pose, like one drawing printed again; the red seal glints in the beam. It does one thing: it lifts one front paw, held out pad up, and keeps it there. A speckled brook trout hangs at the right; a faint turkey reflection low left on the glass leans closer.

Beat 3 (5.5-8s), hard cut across the glass to the outside view of the reference: the riveted metal tin on the pebble riverbed, the turkey small behind the amber porthole. In the foreground the same hound in the identical pose, paw still out, envelope held up level with the brass porthole as if to post it. A stream of bubbles chugs from the back end of the tin. A trout beside the hound looks from the envelope to the tin. Hound alone between two tall weed stalks on a flat strip of ground, staged like the single figure in the last reference.
