# feedstock: the-impossible-voyage | control: lamp | atlas: a07
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass as a hand-painted stage flat seen side-on: a plump shark filling the port, blunt cream snout touching the right edge of the glass, saffron wash and brown crayon spots that overrun their edges; water in horizontal sea-green streaks. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. The shark's near side lifts away and stays off, showing a shallow toy-theatre box. In the front room a hinged row of small cream teeth; in the middle a tiny cream-and-red otter cranks a wheel. The shark never moves; only the insides do, in jerky hand-cranked steps: the teeth open once and close.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same saffron spotted shark, side-on, snout pressed flat on the brass rim, its cutaway side toward us: the middle room is now a rack of small round unlabelled tins. One tin drops from a flap under the chin onto the pebbles. One speckled trout looks at the tin, then up at the shark's round eye, which blinks.

Side-on cutaway box and streaked green water as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
