# feedstock: vesuvius | control: ballast | atlas: a15
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, six-bolt brass porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From frame one the porthole reference fills the glass as a hand-made poster, flat, frontal and shadowless on dusty pink board: a raw potato as one speckled black block upright dead centre, ten bendy straws in brick red, orange and cream sticking out all round like spines, mirror-symmetrical, wobbly hand-ruled edges. Around it sit ruled pencil arcs and rows of ticks that are not letters. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops a small iron weight into the ballast slot and leaves. On each of three slow beats the ten straws hinge at their elbows one notch further down, together, like cut paper, and after each step a new pencil tick draws itself beside every straw tip. The straws stop, pointing down, and hold still.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the amber port. Left foreground: the same poster potato with its ten straws folded down, a row of pencil ticks beside it, and one speckled trout nose to skin with it. The trout looks it up and down, then leans in with its mouth open and stops just short of the skin, where one small round bite is already missing. One straw pops up a notch.

Handled paper grain as in the last reference only. No text, no numbers, no labels, no captions.
