# feedstock: the-impossible-voyage | control: lamp | atlas: a07
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, six-bolt brass porthole, ballast slot lower left, lamp toggle lower right. The porthole reference is in the glass from the first frame, drawn as a hand-painted stage flat seen exactly side-on: a raft of many upside-down glass canning jars lashed together with knotted brown rope, hung from the surface, each jar a small compartment with air at its top, like a toy theatre. Colour is laid by hand over grey tone in washes: saffron on the tin lids, sea green on water painted as horizontal streaks, dull red and cream on the crew. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves; the lids glow saffron. The raft stays fixed like scenery; only the small trout crew move, in jerky hand-cranked steps: one pushes a mismatched jar up into a gap, a second knots a rope round it.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still: the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. Upper left foreground: the same jar raft side-on, a speckled trout beside it. The trout hooks a small bottle against the rim of one tin lid and levers; the cap pops off and sinks. From the next jar a little basket on a string comes down in jerky steps with one button in it.

Cutaway box against streaked green water as in the last reference, none of its figures. No text, no labels, no captions.
