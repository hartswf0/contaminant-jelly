Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, but looking filmed, not drawn, no outlines: one real crimson maple leaf, wet and shiny, sharp and close, upper middle, everything behind melted into blur, the water storm blue falling to wet black. A few thin weed strands hang across the top. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. One hard lamp comes on low behind the leaf: fine falling specks turn into bright threads and the leaf glows through like an ember. It sinks rocking left, right, left in even beats like a pendulum, one notch lower each swing, five swings. On the last swing two smaller red leaves slip in under the strands at the top.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the round amber port. In the left foreground, clear of the port, the same wet crimson leaf comes down from the bright surface, rocking in the same beat, light as a feather, trailing one thin thread of red-lit specks above it. One speckled trout beside it jerks back one length, then eases forward and lets the leaf settle on its nose. Three more red leaves start down from the top edge.

Wet rim light as in the last reference only; no ring, no drop. No text, no labels, no captions.
