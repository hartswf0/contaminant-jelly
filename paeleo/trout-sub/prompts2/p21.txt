# feedstock: one-more-time | control: lamp | atlas: a13
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, six-bolt brass porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From frame one the porthole reference fills the glass as clean cel animation, thin dark-blue contours, flat fills: a sunken yellow bus side-on, and in its five windows five trout heads far too big, each overflowing its window like a cut-out glued on at the wrong scale, each bent over a small tablet. The water is a smooth gradient from emerald through violet to indigo; soft white orbs drift. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. Nothing outside is lit; the five tablets light from within in electric cyan. On a steady beat all five tap at once, four times, and with each tap a thin cyan ring pulses outward from every tablet toward the glass. Then all five heads sway left together once, as if over a bump.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the amber port. Left foreground: the same cel-drawn yellow bus is toy-sized, no longer than the speckled trout beside it, its windows glowing cyan, an orange-gold wind-up key turning on its roof. Its wheels spin in the silt and it does not move. The trout waits beside it, tapping one fin on the same beat, looking away down the river.

Gradient water and soft white orbs as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
