# feedstock: submarines | control: lamp | atlas: a15,a16
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass as a pencil drawing on old sepia paper: a creased map with only river lines hanging open on a thread, thin wobbly dark-brown outlines, rubbed shading, woven grain. On the left panel a scaly turkey claw, on the right a spotted trout fin with rays, no fingers; they clasp exactly on the centre crease, a small olive twig drawn above. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. One pale soft-edged cone fans across the paper. The right panel folds slowly over along the crease and stops halfway: at the new seam the claw now meets a pencilled snare loop, the twig its trigger stick. It holds, then unfolds to the handshake.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same sepia map hangs on its thread, folded along the crease into a standing V so both faces show: on one the claw-and-fin handshake, on the other a flat black trout shape. One speckled trout reaches a fin to the paper fin, then looks at the black face.

Rubbed handled paper and loose brown line as in the last references only. No text, no letters on the map, no labels, no captions.
