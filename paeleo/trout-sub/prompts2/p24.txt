Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, and it looks filmed, not drawn: no outlines, deep storm blue water falling to wet black, one hard lamp far behind turning fine falling specks into bright threads. A chewed green twine net hangs across the port. In its ragged hole a speckled trout, sharp and close, is held back by one last taut strand across its chest. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops a round iron weight into the ballast slot and leaves. The taut strand quivers, then parts in the middle, once; its frayed ends curl slowly apart. The slack net sags toward the glass in a loop over Turkey's faint reflection, and the trout drifts slowly forward through the hole.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the amber port. One strand of green twine runs from the tin's top handle straight up out of frame, taut, like a float line. The net hangs in two cut halves either side of the brass latch. One trout at left foreground looks from the cut ends to the latch. A single severed end sinks slowly.

Pale rim light on dark wet water as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
