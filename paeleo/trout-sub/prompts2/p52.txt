Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot, chalk chart, lamp toggle. From frame one the porthole reference is in the glass as deep blue-green murk full of slowly drifting pale specks: one thick twisted rope, the only fully painted thing, dull red inside a small pool of pale greenish-white light, taut from lower left up to a knot on an iron ring. The stone wall and the boulder pile beyond are only traced in hair-thin wavering pale cyan threads and dots. Past the pool the rope thins to a single thread running on into the dark. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops a grey weight into the slot and leaves. The rope does one thing, slowly: it slackens and sways in a soft S like a limb, then draws straight and hard again,.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. The same rope seen from the river: knotted to the handle on top of the tin, crossing the left foreground, and running on as a thread across a low arched doorway traced in cyan dots. One speckled trout waits inside the doorway, behind the rope. The rope tightens and one thread boulder slides a hand's width, half clear of the doorway, half across it, and settles.

The small haloed pool of light and the single line leaving it as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
