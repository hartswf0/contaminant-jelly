Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, but looking photographed, not drawn, grainy, no outlines: a blank orange plastic picnic cooler with a white lid and spigot, tipped on mossy stones, blue-green haze behind, tall weed stems either side. Clear vinyl tubing loops from the spigot; midway two cut tube ends meet in a lumpy splice of brown twine and a rubber patch, leaking a thread of bubbles. One speckled trout at the right holds the loose twine end in its mouth. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. One amber pool of light lands on the splice; all else sinks to dark teal. The trout slowly pulls the twine tight. The leak stops, and bubbles now pass through the tube in slow even puffs, like breathing, out the far end by the trout's gills.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the round amber port. Low at the far left, clear of the port, the same blank cooler lies on its stones; the loose end of its spliced tube rests against the hull like a drinking straw. One trout presses the white spigot with its nose: one short fizzy jet squirts out.
