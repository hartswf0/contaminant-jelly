# feedstock: one-more-time | control: ballast | atlas: a13
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, drawn as clean cel animation: thin even dark-blue contours, flat fills, water a smooth gradient from emerald to indigo. A heap of coals over a dark crack in the riverbed glows from within, orange-gold, the one warm thing. A straight column of flat ribbons and bubbles rises from it. A speckled trout holds still at the right, fins spread. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops a round iron weight into the ballast slot and leaves. The glow pulses outward in soft rings on a steady beat. Nothing touches the coals; the column does the moving: it lifts one loose weed strand and turns it round and round like a stirred noodle, and its push tips one dark lump off the heap, which rolls toward the glass.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still. The coal heap sits at left foreground inside a ring of stones, its column rising well left of the tin. One trout holds both fins out to it. Behind the amber port Turkey, feathers puffed, presses both wings to the glass toward the glow. Each ring reaches the brass rim and fades.

Flat form on pure gradient with soft orbs as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
