# feedstock: the-rifle-s-spiral | control: lamp | atlas: a36,a35
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass as a photographed tabletop miniature: a television cabinet carved from matte painted wood with visible grain, two wire rabbit ears, on a dusty tan floor, one hard light raking from the side, black beyond. Its screen is the only colour and the only thing that moves: a flat cartoon of a yellow sponge with a face, green algae in its corners. The screen's light lands as one pale rectangle on the floor, over two carved trout lying still. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. Inside the screen the sponge swings a blue cloth high overhead, side to side, three times, like a call for help; then it wipes one green corner clean.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port, like a second round screen. The carved wooden set stands at left foreground, the same sponge waving on its screen. Two carved trout between set and tin turn their heads stiffly, in one step, from the screen to the port, and hold. All motion stays inside the two frames.

Matte carved wood, raking light and pale floor patches as in the last references only. No text, no labels, no captions.
