# feedstock: this-land-is-mine | control: ballast | atlas: a28
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Two shots, one hard cut, locked-off camera in both.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference for the whole shot, riveted steel tin, centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall, all in the house watercolour. From the first frame the scene of the porthole reference is in the glass, but as flat cut paper with clean edges and no shadows: water one smooth gradient, slate blue above to warm peach below; two pale spotted trunks bracket a narrow ground strip of repeated round moss rosettes; two thin old elders in waistcoats stand on it in strict profile, rigid pieces with round brass pins at shoulders, elbows and knees; a carved boundary stone lower right. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters at the lower left corner, drops a grey weight into the slot and leaves. Each elder then swings one straight arm up from its shoulder pin in a clean arc, like a toll gate, and stops dead; the left elder swivels to a paper-thin edge and back. One speckled trout slides across in front. Trunks, strip and stone never move.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the tin in the same place, Turkey small behind the round amber port. The same two trunks stand behind the tin at left and right. In the right foreground the stone now sits one step nearer the tin, leaving one bare pale disc in the rosette row; one flat elder points a pinned arm at the stone, the other holds out a coin toward the tin. In the left foreground one speckled trout looks from stone to tin.

Bracketed stage and flat gradient as in the last reference only; nothing in it appears. No text, no labels, no captions.
