# feedstock: this-land-is-mine | control: ballast | atlas: a28
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Two shots, one hard cut.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, centered brass six-bolt porthole with a chip at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent in the right wall. From the first frame the scene of the porthole reference is in the glass, as flat cut paper with clean edges and no shadows: water one smooth gradient, slate blue down to warm peach; two pale spotted trunks bracket a strip of repeated moss rosettes; on it two thin old elders in waistcoats in strict profile, rigid with round brass pins at shoulders, elbows and knees; a boundary stone lower right. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters at the lower left corner, drops a grey weight into the slot and leaves. Each elder swings one straight arm up from its shoulder pin like a toll gate and stops dead; the left elder swivels to a paper-thin edge and back. Trunks and stone never move.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the tin in the same place, Turkey small behind the amber port, the two trunks behind it. In the right foreground the stone sits one step nearer the tin, leaving one bare disc in the rosette row; one flat elder points at the stone, the other holds a coin out to the tin. At left one speckled trout looks from stone to tin.

Bracketed stage and gradient as in the last reference only; nothing in it appears. No text, no labels, no captions.
