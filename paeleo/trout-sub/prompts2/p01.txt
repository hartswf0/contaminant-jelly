Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, drawn like an old steel engraving: a giant rubber boot standing on the riverbed, a solid black shape, black weed as a near wing at left. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. One pale hard-edged wedge of light opens behind the boot and the water turns pale teal, so the boot stays a pure backlit silhouette; fine ruled hoops round its leg like hull planks. The boot rocks once on its heel, rigid: the toe lifts, holds, and sets down with a puff of silt. While the toe is up, one small black trout silhouette glides level through the arch under the instep.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same engraved black boot towers beside one speckled trout, sole toward us. A pale wedge from the port lights the ridged sole into a bright disc. From a hole in the toe one small claw swings out on its pivot, snaps shut once, and the trout flinches.

Beam wedge and ruled hatching as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
