# feedstock: submarines | control: lamp | atlas: a15,a16
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, drawn as pencil on old rubbed sepia paper: a torn creek map stuck flat on our side of the glass, stained, wavy creek lines, one bold dark X, and a large spiral-shelled snail beside the X, thin wobbly dark-brown outline, smudged shading, no colour. Past the torn edge, grey murk and one small black trout silhouette. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. A pale soft-edged cone fans up the paper. The snail creeps a slow arc round the X, its wet trail a pale ring, then settles its shell exactly on the X so no stroke shows. A small notch is left in the paper at its mouth.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small, peeking past the torn map that covers the amber port from inside. Pressed to the glass, backlit, the shell is one dark spiral disc, no body showing. In the left foreground one speckled trout beside a round river pebble the same size, looking from pebble to port. The disc slides slowly sideways like an eye turning, then stops dead.

Paper grain and rubbed shading as in the last two references only. No text, no labels, no captions.
