Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Two shots, one hard cut, locked-off camera in both.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, looking like a photographed tabletop miniature under water, no drawn outlines: a mossy boulder arch, a small brass bell hung from a twig at its top, a knotted cord on the clapper, fine gravel below, teal haze behind, one speckled trout like smooth painted clay sharp at the right of the bell. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters at the lower right corner, flips the lamp toggle and leaves; an amber beam rises from below and glints on the bell. The trout swims one slow full clockwise circle around the bell, over the top and back under, then nudges the knot with its nose. The bell swings wide once. The trout freezes, mouth open.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same brass bell hangs from its twig, and one speckled trout beside it holds the cord's knot in its mouth, facing the port. It bows its head, then tugs once; the bell swings and Turkey startles.

Staging as in the last reference: one fish close beside a small hung bell over a stony bottom. No text, no labels, no captions.
