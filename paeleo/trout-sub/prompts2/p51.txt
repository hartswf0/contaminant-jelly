# feedstock: the-rifle-s-spiral | control: lamp | atlas: a36
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot, chalk chart, lamp toggle. From frame one the porthole reference is in the glass as a photographed tabletop miniature: a giant clam carved from wood and painted matte, grain and cut grooves instead of ink lines, blue-black and slate on a dusty tan floor. Its shells are shut to a thin gap; a bone-pale glow leaks out and lies as a patch on the floor. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. The same look happens twice. The upper shell hinges up stiffly in three small steps and holds: a bone-white pearl as big as a basketball, smooth and egg-shaped, rocks once. The shell steps shut. It steps open again the same way: now the pearl sits tight like a stopper and one line of bubbles leaks past its edge.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same carved clam stands open, the pearl the one warm thing, glinting dull brass. One speckled trout has its nose on the pearl. The pearl rolls a finger's width toward the trout and rolls back; the trout holds still. The upper shell drops one step and stops.

Matte carved wood, seams, raking side light and tan floor as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
