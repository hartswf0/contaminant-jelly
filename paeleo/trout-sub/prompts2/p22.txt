# feedstock: water-fountain | control: ballast | atlas: a32
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, six-bolt brass porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From frame one the porthole reference fills the glass as a handmade stage set of flat matt cut card and felt under even shadowless light: three speckled trout upright shoulder to shoulder across the whole width of the glass, fins straight out in front, facing a larger coach trout at left with a tomato-red whistle cord, all with round stuck-on eyes, on a flat turquoise ground crossed by columns of short identical lilac bars. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops a ring-handled iron weight into the ballast slot and leaves. On the clunk the coach snaps one nod and the loop starts on a steady beat: the three snap into a squat, hold, snap up, hold, four times, each repeat identical. On every down beat one whole bubble pops on above each head.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the amber port. The tin sits handle up, like a big weight. Left foreground, seen from behind: the same cut-card coach and three trout keep the same loop, squat and rise, exactly together, facing the tin. On each beat Turkey's wing tip clicks down behind the glass and the coach's fin taps the tin's handle.

Flat ground with columns of short bars as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
