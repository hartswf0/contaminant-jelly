# feedstock: pyramid-song | control: lamp | atlas: a19
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Two shots, one hard cut.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the glass holds the porthole reference in deep blue-green murk full of slow pale specks: a stone bridge arch traced only in hair-thin pale cyan threads and dots, and under it six large glass bubbles in a level row, each moored to the bed by one thin thread, each holding one capital letter: A, E, I, O, U, Y. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. One small pool of pale greenish-white light then travels along the row left to right; each bubble it reaches turns golden and stays lit, except the second, E, which stays a thin unlit thread ring.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still: the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. The same row floats in the left foreground beside one speckled trout: A, I, O, U, Y golden, E still a thread ring. Turkey, wholly behind the glass, taps the inside of the port once with its beak; the port blinks amber once like a buzzer, and the unlit E bubble alone pops into a few specks. The five others glow on to the last frame. The trout stares at the beak.

The only lettering is the six letters A E I O U Y in the bubbles; no other text, no labels, no captions.
