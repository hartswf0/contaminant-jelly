Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass as flat layers like cut paper washed with watercolour, no outlines: a big round disc facing us dead centre, cream with one near-black navy spiral, pinned at its hub to a rust-red A-frame stand; behind it rounded bands of indigo and ice blue. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. The disc turns steadily about its hub, always flat to the glass, never edge-on. Along the spiral thin chalk-pale dots and curls ride round and brighten one after another from rim to hub, so the disc seems to sink into a tunnel. The water bands slide sideways and bend in toward the hub. One speckled trout drifts toward the hub.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same cream-and-navy spiral disc, flat toward us, still turning. One speckled trout flicks the rim with its tail; a small brass peg at the top ticks and the disc slows and stops. A striped sock lies flat on the disc's face; soft-haloed white orbs float up from the hub like suds.

Sliding rounded blue bands and haloed orbs as in the last reference only. No text, no letters, no labels, no captions.
