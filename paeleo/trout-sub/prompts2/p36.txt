# feedstock: this-land-is-mine | control: lamp | atlas: a28
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass as flat cut paper, clean edges, no shadows: water one smooth gradient, slate blue down to warm peach; a strip of repeated moss rosettes; two leaflet weed trees bracket a slatted bench; before it a brown hound sits in strict profile, a rigid cutout pinned at shoulder and hip, a white envelope with one red wax seal in its mouth. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. The hound is one drawing held again and again; only one piece changes at a time. Its front leg swings out from the shoulder pin, pad up, and stops dead; then the envelope's flap swings half open on its pin and stops.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground, between the same two paper trees, the identical hound cutout in the identical pose, paw still out, envelope level with the brass port like a letter at a slot. One speckled trout beside it looks from the seal to the tin. A row of bubbles puffs from the tin's back end.

Bracketed stage and gradient as in the last reference only; nothing in it appears. No text, no labels, no captions.
