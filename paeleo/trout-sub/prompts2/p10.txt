# feedstock: water-fountain | control: lamp | atlas: a32
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Two shots, one hard cut, locked-off camera in both.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, steel tin, centered brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent on the right, in the house watercolour. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, rebuilt as a handmade stage set of flat matt cut card: a wall of flat pink brick blocks at left, a flat turquoise ground at right crossed by columns of short lemon bars, and a big white ribbed clam fixed to the wall by one round brass pin at its back, shut, three small cut-card trout below it. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters at the lower right corner, flips the toggle and leaves. Then on a steady beat the top half alone snaps up on the pin, holds, and claps shut, three times; the bottom half and the pin never move. Each clap adds one white ring to the right and the three trout hop back one step, then step back in.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the tin on the pebble riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same cut-card clam on its pink brick wall, shut. Three speckled trout pass beneath it in single file. The last one stops, noses the top half up on its pin, peeks in, and the lid drops shut.

Flat ground and bars as in the last reference; no people. No text, no labels, no captions.
