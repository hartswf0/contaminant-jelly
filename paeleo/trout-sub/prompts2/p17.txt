# feedstock: this-land-is-mine | control: ballast | atlas: a28
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, six-bolt brass porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From frame one the porthole reference fills the glass as flat cut paper, clean edges, no shadow: five stern speckled trout in round spectacles, in strict profile around a mossy round stone table with blank slates and pebble stacks, on a ground strip of one repeated round motif, the water a gradient from slate blue to warm peach. Each trout is rigid pieces hinged at visible round pins. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops a small iron weight into the ballast slot and leaves. On the clink four trout swing one fin from its pin in one clean arc, rap the stone and stop dead, three times, exactly together. The middle trout alone swerves: its fin stays down, its head turns on its pin to face straight out of the glass, and it holds that stare.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the amber port. Turkey stands upright and still. Left foreground: the same paper table and four council trout in profile, facing the tin. The middle trout, beside the tin, swings its pinned fin once and sets one pebble on the riverbed directly under the porthole, then looks up and waits. The amber lamp blinks once.

Flat stage and gradient as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
