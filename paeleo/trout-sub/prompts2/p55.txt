# feedstock: something-in-the-kitchen | control: lamp | atlas: a44
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot, chalk chart, lamp toggle. From frame one the porthole reference is in the glass as a photographed miniature, not a drawing: real brass, cork and glass, cool white light from above, violet dusk behind. A corked brass-capped glass tube full of rolled pastel postcards hangs large and close in a slow eddy. Small and deep behind it, a tidy grid of small colour squares. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. The eddy carries the tube once round. As it passes closest one card slides half out past the cork and opens flat against the port like a notice on a door: a tiny drawing of a brass porthole with a tiny corked tube inside it, over three wavy ink lines that are not letters.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same tube stands upright on the riverbed and one speckled trout faces it at arm's length, the loose card held between them in the trout's mouth. The trout pushes the card back in beside the cork like posting a letter, lets go and stares at the tube, which glints pale green. The tube begins the same circle again.

Two parties at hand distance, one object between them, as in the last reference only. No text, no letters, no labels, no captions.
