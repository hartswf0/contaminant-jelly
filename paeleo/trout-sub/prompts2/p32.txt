# feedstock: this-land-is-mine | control: ballast | atlas: a28
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the glass holds the porthole reference as flat cut paper in strict profile, no shadows: water one smooth gradient, slate blue to peach; a floor strip of one repeated round pebble; two leaflet weeds bracketing the view; and, from a slot in a yellow hull at the left edge, a brass scissor-jack arm half folded, every crossing a visible round pin, its two-jaw claw pinching an iron skeleton key. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops a small iron weight into the slot and leaves. The arm unfolds link by link, each bar swinging in a clean arc from its pin and stopping dead. The claw snaps open and shut once like a crab pincer, catching the key again, then turns the key a quarter turn as if in a lock.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still: the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. The same long pinned arm reaches from a slot in the tin's left end, never from the port, into the left foreground. One speckled trout gapes and the claw brings the key tip to its teeth. Beside that trout, from the first frame, sits a small padlocked tackle box, the trout's fin on its lid.

No people. No text, no labels, no captions.
