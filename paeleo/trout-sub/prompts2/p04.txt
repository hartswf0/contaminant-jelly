# feedstock: where-the-river-meets-the-road | control: lamp | atlas: a48
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, looking filmed, not drawn: deep storm-blue water falling to wet black, an old stone bridge arch melted into soft blur behind, thin moss strands hanging across the view, and close and sharp under it one low dark wet mossy dome, no head, no legs, one brass glint at its lower edge. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. One hard pale lamp comes on behind: a thin bright ring runs round the wet rim and falling specks turn to bright threads. Very slowly a blunt head slides out only as far as the shell rim, one pale eye catching the light, holds, and slides back in. One bubble rises.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground, wet and sharp, the same snapping turtle beside one speckled trout, head and one foreleg out, a claw hooked through the ring of a large brass key. It draws the key's teeth once, slowly, along the mossy edge of its shell, then pulls head, leg and key in under the rim.

Rim light as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
