# feedstock: submarines | control: lamp | atlas: a17,a15
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Two shots, one hard cut, locked-off camera in both.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, riveted steel tin, centered brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent on the right, in the house watercolour. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, drawn as pencil on old rubbed sepia paper: grainy grey-brown murk and a solid black spiked star on a rope tied to a stone, ringed by eight small black trout silhouettes facing it. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters at the lower right corner, flips the toggle and leaves. One pale soft-edged cone fans up from below the port and inside it the star shows thin wobbly brown outlines of paper fringe rows and ribbon tails, no colour. The trout hum one note that slides upward; as it climbs the whole ring rises slowly and level, and the star lifts until its rope pulls taut.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same fringed star hangs on its taut rope, drawn in sepia pencil, one speckled trout beside it holding a short plain brass tube in its mouth. It buzzes bubbles through the tube as the note slides down, then swings the tube up toward the star and stops short.

Cone and grain as in the last two references. No text, no labels, no captions.
