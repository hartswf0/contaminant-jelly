# feedstock: the-impossible-voyage | control: lamp | atlas: a07
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, six-bolt brass porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From frame one the porthole reference fills the glass as a hand-painted stage flat seen exactly side-on: a second small steel tin submarine with copper pipes, its near wall removed so the inside shows as a shallow box like a toy theatre, a tiny turkey at a tiny seat by a tiny toggle and an unlit round port. Saffron wash on its metal, sea-green streaked water, wobbling edges. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves; an amber beam comes on below our port. Exactly one second later the tiny turkey, in jerky hand-cranked steps, flips its own toggle and its port lights saffron. Then it waves one wing twice. The twin never moves; only the small figure inside does.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the amber port. Left foreground: the same painted twin sits nose to nose against our tin, overlapping it, so the two read as one long steel body with two round amber windows. Turkey lifts one wing; one second later the tiny turkey in the twin's port lifts the same wing. One speckled trout looks from window to window; a second, blocked behind the twin, backs away.

Cutaway box and streaked green water as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
