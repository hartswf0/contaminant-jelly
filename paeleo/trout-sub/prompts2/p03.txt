Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Two shots, one hard cut, locked-off camera in both.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, riveted steel tin, centered brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent on the right, in the house watercolour. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, shown as a small built model filmed under water, no outlines: a wood-framed slate chalkboard on fine sand, a blurred mossy stone close, green haze behind. Thin dusty chalk reads HOOK MONSTER, unchanged for the whole film, beside a chalk sketch of a plump fan-tailed bird hanging on a fish hook. Above the words the heading is only a pale washed smear. A small empty stool stands before the board. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters at the lower right corner, flips the toggle and leaves. Soft light bands slide over the sand; a ribbon of current crosses the smear and carries chalk dust off in a milky trail.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same chalkboard, smear on top, with only two small young trout wearing satchels: one holds a worm up to the glass, the other ducks behind the board frame and points a fin from the sketch to Turkey.

Light bands and low pupils as in the last two references. The only lettering is HOOK MONSTER on the chalkboard; no other text, no labels, no captions.
