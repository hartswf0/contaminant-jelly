Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, six-bolt brass porthole, ballast slot lower left, lamp toggle lower right. The porthole reference is in the glass from the first frame, looking like a small built model filmed under water: rounded forms, no outline, glossy skin, fine sand. A rusty orange diamond road sign stands planted in river gravel, the only crisp surface, lettered DAM in plain dark capitals. In front of it hovers one three-inch tan darter. Four large trout wait in a line behind the sign in green haze. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. Daylight slides in slow rippled bands across the sand, and the tiny darter's single long shadow stretches away over the sand until it is wider than the sign and lies across all four trout, who step forward, meet it and stop.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still: the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. Scale reversed: the darter is nearest and sharp, left foreground, larger in frame than the tin, beside one small speckled trout. Head down, it shoves a low ridge of pebbles forward with its snout like a blade; a fresh groove runs back from it to the foot of the DAM sign, now small in the far haze.

Light bands on sand and the long shadow as in the last reference, none of its figures. The only lettering is the word DAM on the sign; no other text, no labels, no captions.
