# feedstock: song-of-the-sea | control: lamp | atlas: a39
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Two shots, one hard cut.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the glass holds the porthole reference as flat cut-paper layers washed with watercolour, speckled, no dark outlines: a rounded mossy arch, water in wide bands of night indigo, slate and ice blue sliding slowly sideways, and one ribbon of hundreds of silver minnows in a big S, each minnow a chalk-pale dash, none outside the band. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. The dashes then brighten one after another from tail to head. The tail of the ribbon curls once round a riverbed stone and draws tight; the head stays a sharp arrowhead pointing up right into the arch.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still: the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. Behind it the same paper arch. The same S ribbon, still one band with no strays, passes one speckled trout in the left foreground and files into a line behind the tin, so the tin sits at its head like the first float of a parade, glinting the same chalk silver as the minnows. The trout looks from the tin to the minnows.

Flat sliding bands as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
