Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, drawn like an old steel engraving: a little theatre of flat cardboard cut-outs, five scalloped curtain arches one inside the next like ribs, each smaller and paler, shaded only by fine ruled parallel lines in black, grey and white. In the plank floor under the last arch is one round hole, the only colour: bright green. A black trout silhouette waits in the right wing. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. The arches part one after another, nearest first, each sliding flat and rigid sideways, so the green hole stands open at the end of the ribs. A flat paper leaf glides in a level circle round the hole and drops in.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the round amber port. At the far left, clear of the port, the same stage is seen exactly side-on: five hatched arches in a row, thin as card. From the floor hole one hard-edged green wedge points straight up. One speckled trout drops a pebble into a tin cup at the stage's foot, glides into the green wedge and turns to face out. A second trout stares at Turkey.
