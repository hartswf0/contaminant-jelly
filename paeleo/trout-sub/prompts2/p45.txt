# feedstock: dashboard | control: ballast | atlas: a22
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass looking photographed, not drawn: a tabletop miniature at table height, shallow focus, a real plastic funnel hanging sideways on two threads. Colour is drained to slate and bone white; only the funnel is dull ember orange. A glass baster with a rubber bulb sits tip-first in the wide mouth. One soft warm side light; film grain. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops a grey weight into the slot and leaves. In small deliberate steps, the baster is drawn into the mouth and shortens to nothing, while bone-white pellets leave the narrow brass spout one at a time. Last, a riveted scrap of grey tin plate on the riverbed tips up toward the mouth and holds.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same orange funnel on its threads, still sideways, wide mouth toward the tin. Bone-white pellets rise from the spout in a line like bubbles and one speckled trout noses one. Under the wide mouth grains of gravel lift off the riverbed in a thin line.

Table-height lens, sharp near object and soft dark behind as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
