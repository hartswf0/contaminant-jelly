From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, as a photographed tabletop miniature: no outlines, smooth painted-clay forms, matte grey cave rock, soft top light, teal haze, shallow focus. A freckled trout walks upright on its tail fins along a rock ledge, wearing a riveted brass collar and, on its head, a round glass fishbowl, open rim up, full of water to a clear waterline. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves; one small warm glint lands on the brass collar. The trout stops mid-step, turns slowly to face the glass and blinks. A single line of bubbles rises inside the bowl and stops at the waterline. Outside the bowl nothing drifts: no bubble, no speck.
