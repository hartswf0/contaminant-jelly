Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, looking photographed, grainy colour film, no outline: a waterline exactly across the middle of the glass. Above, air: orange maple leaves and a bank. Below, teal water, pale sun shafts dropping from the rippled surface, reed stems fading into haze. Two grey furry raccoon forearms reach down from the top holding a round ribbed tin disc, half in air, half in water; the wet half sits shifted a little sideways from the dry half. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops an iron weight into the slot and leaves; the waterline shivers once. The paws turn the disc one slow quarter turn like a wheel, rub its rim, then lift it a hand's width and hold.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground one speckled trout looks straight up. Above it, at the top left edge, the bright rippled surface seen from below, the tin disc floating flat on it, bubbles fizzing from under its rim, two blurred dark paws wavering beyond.

Sun through the surface as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
