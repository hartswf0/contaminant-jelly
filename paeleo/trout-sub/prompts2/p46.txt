# feedstock: water-fountain | control: ballast | atlas: a31
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, six-bolt brass porthole, ballast slot lower left, lamp toggle lower right. The porthole reference is in the glass from the first frame, built as a handmade stage set: six thick block letters cut from matt lemon-yellow card, brass-gold faced, stand as a staircase climbing left to right, one letter per step, G, O, B, B, L, E, on flat green felt steps against one flat turquoise ground. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters lower left, drops one small grey lead weight into the ballast slot and leaves. Then, on a steady beat, each letter in turn snaps up a little and back, G, O, B, B, L, one per beat. On the sixth beat the top E snaps sideways off its step and drops straight down in whole steps, one per beat, heavy as lead.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still: the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. Left foreground, beside one speckled trout: the same six card letters as stairs, G O B B L in place, the fallen E lying flat on the gravel at their foot. The trout hops up one letter per beat, two steps, then stops and looks down at the E; the staircase leans one notch toward it, like a sign coming loose.

Flat matt blocks as in the last reference, none of its objects. The only lettering is the six letters G O B B L E; no other text, no labels, no captions.
