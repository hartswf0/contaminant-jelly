Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference for the whole shot, riveted steel tin, centered brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, in the house watercolour. From the first frame the scene of the porthole reference is in the glass, drawn as flat grainy cut-paper layers with no dark outlines: a tall pointed window with an arch of coloured pebbles and a flower rosette, six speckled trout in two facing rows before it, water in wide rounded bands of indigo and pale blue sliding sideways, one flat white shaft slanting through the panes. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters at the lower right corner, flips the toggle and leaves. At once the white shaft fans into five flat patches, red, gold, green, blue, violet, lying side by side on the riverbed. Each trout takes the colour of its patch; all six open their mouths together and chalk-pale rings and dots rise from them.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the round amber port, one wing raised. The pebble-arched window stands at far left; its five colour patches lie across the tin and the brass rim shines gold. One trout at left foreground looks in. Pale rings leave the rim; small haloed orbs float up.
