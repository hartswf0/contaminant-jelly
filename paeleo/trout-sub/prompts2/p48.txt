# feedstock: song-of-the-sea | control: ballast | atlas: a39
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, six-bolt brass porthole, ballast slot lower left, lamp toggle lower right. The porthole reference is in the glass from the first frame, drawn as flat stacked layers like cut paper washed with grainy watercolour, no outlines, no shadows: a waterline cuts the port in half. Above it, pale ice-blue air and the pointed roof of a tin steeple with a plain arrow weathervane, no letters on it. Below it, wide rounded bands of slate and night indigo slide sideways, and the steeple's open arch holds a dark round bell. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters lower left, drops one small grey weight into the ballast slot and leaves. Below it the bell swings once and thin chalk-pale rings brighten one after another, spreading through the dark bands and stopping dead at the waterline. Above it nothing is drawn: only the arrow making one quarter turn.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still: the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. Left foreground, beside one speckled trout: the same paper steeple, bell low, roof and arrow rising into a pale surface band at the top edge. The bell swings again; chalk-pale rings spread through the water and the trout, while up in the pale band the arrow turns silently.

Sliding rounded bands of blue as in the last reference. No text, no letters, no labels, no captions.
