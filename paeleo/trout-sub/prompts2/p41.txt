From the first frame the bream of the porthole reference stands in the glass: a pale pink sea bream in black sunglasses on two thin wooden stick legs, but as a grainy soft grey-pink photographed cutout with a thin dark outline round it. On the third step it snaps in one frame into a flat drawing of angular facets, ice blue and cobalt in broken pencil, the river behind straight horizontal white-blue streaks and the legs gone; one step later it snaps back, solid, and keeps walking.
