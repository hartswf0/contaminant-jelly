# feedstock: mind-game-trailer | control: lamp | atlas: a26
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot, chalk chart, lamp toggle. From frame one the porthole reference is in the glass: under a heavy rock slab on a steep diagonal lies a wrinkled brown shape, a grainy soft photographed cutout with a thin dark outline traced loosely round it. Still, it reads as a ruffled gravy boat: curled tail for a handle, blunt end for a spout, a frilly rim. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. In a single frame, a cut, never a fade, the same body snaps into a flat drawing of angular facets, pale ice blue where lit, cobalt in shadow, the water behind it straight horizontal white-blue streaks: an eye open on the spout, a webbed foot on the gravel, frilly folds flared like a dragon's ruff. It holds a second and snaps back to the still cutout, eye shut.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same grainy cutout salamander lies beside one speckled trout, both facing the tin, its loose throat folds hanging like the red wattle of the turkey in the port. It snaps once to blue facets over white streaks, folds flared, then snaps back and draws its head under its own folds like a shell.

Steep diagonal of a heavy mass as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
