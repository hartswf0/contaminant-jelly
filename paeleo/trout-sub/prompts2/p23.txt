# feedstock: water-fountain | control: lamp | atlas: a31
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, built as a handmade set of flat matt cut card and felt under even shadowless light, no ink outline: a round cream clock face with twelve plain tick marks on a brown post, ringed by bubblegum pink felt tentacles, on a flat dusk lilac ground crossed by columns of short identical bars. A small speckled trout hovers at the three. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves. Then, on a steady beat, the tentacles are the dial: they snap shut over the face like a mouth, hold, snap open, four times. On the fourth beat both clock hands snap to point right at the trout, and it darts out past the rim.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the amber port. At left foreground the cut-card clock on its post; a short brass chain runs from its rim to the fin of an old trout in round spectacles. Three fry with satchels stand in a row facing the tin. On the beat the tentacles snap shut and open; the old trout taps the face; the fry turn to the tin together.

One object held dead centre on flat colour as in the last reference only. No numerals, no text, no labels, no captions.
