# feedstock: vesuvius | control: lamp | atlas: a15
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images. Two shots, one hard cut, locked-off camera in both.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, riveted steel tin, centered brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, empty ballast slot lower left, chalk chart lower center, brass lamp toggle lower right, amber lamp overhead, dent on the right. From the first frame the port is filled by a hand-made poster in a rough wooden frame: flat, shadowless dusty pink board, and on it an upright symmetrical domed saucer in matte speckled black, brick red and pencil orange blocks, a round window and a hinge on its side, ringed by ruled arrows and rows of pencil ticks. At 1s only a feathered wing tip enters at the lower right corner, flips the toggle and leaves. The fills flicker. In steps, like cut paper, three red arrows slide in and stop at the window and hinge; then four thick black dashed lines draw themselves across the saucer, stroke by stroke, cutting it into five parts.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the tin on the riverbed, the port unobstructed, Turkey small behind the amber glass. At the far left foreground the same framed pink poster stands on two posts, a large paper tag with a drawn empty hourglass hanging from its corner. One speckled trout sets a pebble at its foot and bows; a second taps the tag with a fin.

Handled board as in the last reference. The marks are ticks and arrows, never letters. No text, no labels, no captions.
