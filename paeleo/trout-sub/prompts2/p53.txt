# feedstock: vesuvius | control: ballast | atlas: a15
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot, chalk chart, lamp toggle. From frame one the porthole reference is in the glass as a hand-made poster: flat, frontal, shadowless, on dusty pink board in speckled black, brick red, orange and cream. A mill wheel stands upright in the centre: compass circle, eight ruled black spokes, brick-red paddle blocks on the rim. Under it a cream trapdoor with one orange latch. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops a grey weight into the slot and leaves. The wheel turns forward in steps, one paddle per step; each leaves one pencil tick below, a crimped row of ticks, never letters. On the fourth step a paddle meets the latch and stops dead. The wheel steps back one paddle; the trapdoor hinges up a hand's width, holds, and drops flat.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, the port clear, Turkey small behind the amber port. In the left foreground the same poster wheel is seen end-on beside one speckled trout: a narrow upright stack of black and red blocks, its orange axle hub sticking out toward the trout like a winding key, one brown block wedged between two paddles. The hub turns one click forward, one back, one forward, and the stack rocks against the wedged block.

Handled stained paper as in the last reference only. No text, no labels, no captions.
