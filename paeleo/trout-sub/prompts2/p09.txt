# feedstock: on-melancholy-hill | control: ballast | atlas: a06
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right. From the first frame the porthole reference is in the glass, drawn as flat cut-outs with a dark even contour and flat fills: a rusty wire shopping cart of dented blank cans on a cracked road with yellow dashes, one speckled trout gripping its dark-red handle. The water is hazy teal without outlines, near-black far off, where three pairs of small ice-blue lamps hang in a row. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower left, drops an iron weight into the slot and leaves. Then the creek drags the wrong way: weed, silt and bubbles all stream right to left, and the current pulls the cart nose first to the left while the trout, still gripping the handle, is towed flat behind it like a flag, in three held poses. The lamp pairs glide evenly the same way.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still, Turkey small behind the round amber port. No lamp beam. In the left foreground the same cut-out cart has stopped with its wire nose an inch from the tin, the one trout beside it. The trout frowns at the tin, then lifts one can from the cart, taps the steel with it once and listens. No other fish.

Far lamp pairs in cold haze as in the last reference only; nothing in it appears. No text, no labels, no captions.
