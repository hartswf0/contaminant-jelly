# feedstock: dashboard | control: lamp | atlas: a22
Hand-inked watercolor storybook animation in the ink line, paper grain and palette of the reference images.

Shot 1 (0-5.5s), Turkey's seat, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the cockpit reference throughout, in the house watercolour: steel tin, brass six-bolt porthole chipped at two o'clock, ballast slot lower left, chalk chart, lamp toggle lower right, dent on the right. From the first frame the glass holds the porthole reference looking photographed, not drawn: a tabletop miniature at table height, a real thick photo album already lying open on mossy stones, its cloth cover flap slack like the mouth of a sack, sharp in front, all behind soft and falling to black, colour almost drained to brown, slate and bone, fine grain. At 1s only a wing tip enters lower right, flips the toggle and leaves; one soft warm light comes on from the side. The album never moves. Sepia snapshots of small cabins and a covered bridge lift out one at a time, as if moved by hand, and drift up right in one slow line, like letters leaving a bag.

Shot 2 (5.5-8s), Trout's side, hard cut, locked-off camera, the camera never moves: exactly the outside reference, held still: the tin on the riverbed, Turkey small behind the amber port. Turkey is perfectly still in the round brass rim like a portrait. In the left foreground the same open album lies beside one speckled trout, snapshots still leaving it one at a time toward the tin. One settles on the gravel just below the port, not covering it. The port gives one white flash like a flashbulb. The trout stares at the port.

Focus and drained colour as in the last reference only; no people. No text, no labels, no captions.
