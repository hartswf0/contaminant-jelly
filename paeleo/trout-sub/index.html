Trout Sub · Double Duck/Rabbit Deck · PAE-09

55 portholes, one film each

Every film holds one room: Turkey flips the lamp or fits ballast, the thing arrives at the glass, and the cut crosses to the trout looking in. Each was written by a director, generated once, and checked by a second reader who had not seen the prompt.

Start here: the activity in six shots

  1. Take Turkey’s seat. Oval bread-roll tin, chipped porthole, ballast slot left, chalk chart center, lamp toggle right.
  2. Operate one part and watch the port. Play any porthole film: the lamp or the ballast changes what is outside the glass.
  3. Read both sides of the cut. Turkey writes odd faces (1·3·5, looking out). Trout writes even faces (2·4·6, looking in). Probable, Plausible, Preposterous.
  4. Roll 1d6 and pass the record. Circle the outcome, mark the chart under glass, hand the clue to the next pilot.
