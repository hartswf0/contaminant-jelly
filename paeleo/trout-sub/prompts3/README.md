# Shots for the SCION swarms: people doing it

These are written and waiting. fal's balance is exhausted, so none has been rendered. Each is one 8-second
shot for `tools/portfilm.py gen <id> --prompt-file prompts3/<id>.txt --duration 8 --still none`, in the house
watercolour, with the same two masters as every porthole film. They replace the cut-paper figures in
`tools/explainer.py` one for one.

| id | Shot | What must be visible |
|---|---|---|
| room4-open | Room 4 in Trout Town. Three trout pupils with satchels at one desk under a chalkboard. Through the classroom's round window, the riveted tin with an amber port drifts into view. | pupils turn from the board to the window together |
| room4-slip | Close on the desk. A fin slides one round slip with a number across to the nearest pupil, who lays it beside a blank sheet with a ring on it. | slip, sheet, one pupil is the pilot |
| pilot-draws | The pilot pupil looks up at the window, then draws inside the ring with a pencil held in a fin. In a thought cloud above its head: the turkey's face behind glass. | eyes go window, paper, window |
| pass-left | The pilot pushes the sheet to the pupil on its left. The slip travels on top of the sheet. | the paper and slip move together |
| friend-turkey | The first friend frowns at the slip. Thought cloud: two round buttons, a green triangle and a red circle; the circle glows. The friend draws a circle, then writes. | choosing, then drawing the chosen shape |
| friend-trout | The next friend. Thought cloud: a blue cross and a pink square; the cross glows. Writes three lines; a die face sits beside each. | three lines, dice 4, 5, 6 |
| roll | The sheet returns to the pilot. A fin rolls one die across the desk; it stops on five. All three pupils lean in. The pilot circles a die on the sheet. | one roll, one circle |
| tell | The pilot stands and reads from the sheet to the room. Thought cloud becomes a postcard that floats up and out of the round window toward the tin. | reading aloud, message leaving |
| turn-about | Cut to Turkey's seat in the tin: on the console lies the same sheet, half filled, and a wing tip holds a pencil. Through the port, three trout pupils wave. | the same sheet on both sides of the glass |

Rules for every shot: locked-off camera; no text on screen except the slip's two-digit number; pupils are original
Trout Town trout; describe everything directly and name no film, show or artist.
