Inference from stills and captions only. Things look placed by hand, one at a time, on a still table. The exterior probably rocks gently against a drifting backdrop while stains and scratches flicker.

## LAW
Through the glass the view looks photographed, not drawn: a tabletop miniature built from real small objects and shot at table height with shallow focus, the nearest things sharp, everything behind them soft, the far distance falling to black. Colour is almost drained: tobacco brown, weathered grey, slate blue, bone white, with one small warm accent. One soft warm light comes from the side and slightly above. Fine film grain, faint scratches and mottled stains lie over the picture, darker toward the edge of the glass. Motion is small and deliberate, one object at a time, as if moved by hand.

## Fits
Suits stacks, models and household things whose scale is the puzzle, anything read by its material (sugar, tape, tin, wood), slow rewinds and placements. Does not suit subjects whose bright colour is the clue, fast crowds, or flat signs and diagrams.
