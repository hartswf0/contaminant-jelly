- **Line.** Figures carry a dark, even, slightly angular contour with flat cel fills and one hard shadow shape per form. Set pieces have no outline at all, only shading.
- **Palette.** Deep cyan-teal water (#0E4A5C to #1B7C8E), near-black teal shadow (#0A2A33), a warm dark red for the near vehicle and trim (#B5332A, #7A1F1C), bone white on drawn figures (#EDE6D2), navy stripe (#1F2A4A), and small ice-blue lamps (#BFE9FF).

- **Depth.** Fog layers: near objects saturated and sharp, far ones reduced to lamp pairs and silhouettes.
- **Texture.** Diamond-plate and mesh metal, cables, a slight soft-focus video bloom and edge fringing.

## LAW
Through the glass, draw every creature and object as a flat cut-out with a dark even contour, flat fills and one hard shadow shape, moving in a few held poses. Set it in deep, hazy teal water that is soft-shaded and outline-free, darkening to near-black with distance. Far things are only silhouettes carrying pairs of small ice-blue lamps with soft halos, gliding slowly and evenly. One diagonal shaft of pale light crosses the murk. Keep one warm dark-red accent near the glass against the cold blue. Low key, quiet, slow.

## Fits
Suits portholes about distant lights, convoys, something approaching in murk, scale and distance puzzles, lamp-toggle rows, and two-figure framings in a round window. Does not suit bright daylit shallows, chalkboards or lettering, archival paper subjects, or fast gags.
