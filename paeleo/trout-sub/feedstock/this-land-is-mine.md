# Feedstock · This Land Is Mine

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Flat digital cutout: clean-edged shapes with flat or pattern fills, built like jointed paper puppets.
- **Line.** Almost none. Shapes carry a hair-thin rim of their own colour; the only true line is a black staff. Small circles mark hinges at elbows, knees and wrists (a28, a29).
- **Palette.** Sky as one smooth vertical gradient: slate blue (#4F6A9B) to peach (#E3A572) in a28/a29, paler grey-mauve to pink in a27. Olive leaf (#8E9B52, #5F6B33), tan trunk (#A89060), sand ground (#CDBFA0) to brown-olive (#6E6240), mauve rosettes (#8E5FA6), black (#111111).
- **Light.** None cast: no shadows, no highlights.
- **Staging.** Strict profile, full figure, feet on a narrow strip of repeated rosettes at the bottom edge; two trees bracket the stage (a28, a29).
- **Depth.** By scale only: rows of identical trees shrinking toward pale flat mountains (a27).
- **Texture.** Ornament instead: repeated leaflets on curved stems, scale-arc trunks.

## How it moves
Inference from stills and captions. The hinge circles imply limbs that rotate rigidly about pins. Between a28 and a29 trees, strip and gradient are identical while a second figure has arrived and an arm has swung out, so the stage is locked and only the pieces change. Nothing suggests squash, blur or camera travel.

## LAW
Through the glass everything is flat cut paper with clean edges and no cast shadow: plain or patterned fills, each shape rimmed by a hair-thin line of its own colour. The water is one smooth gradient, slate blue above sinking to warm peach. Figures stand in strict profile on a narrow ground strip of one repeated round motif, bracketed by two ornamental trees built from repeated leaflets and scale-patterned trunks. Bodies are rigid pieces hinged at visible round pins; a limb swings in a clean arc from its pin and stops dead. The stage never changes; only the pieces on it do.

## Fits
Suits two-party standoffs, toll, border and ownership gags, puppets and signs, "thing or cutout" subjects, and rules where fixed ground takes a new mark. Does not suit murk, lamp-reveal portholes, soft creatures, fluid or smoke, deep crowds, or anything needing shadow.
