# Feedstock · This Land Is Mine

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Flat digital cutout: clean-edged shapes with flat or pattern fills, assembled like jointed paper puppets. No brush, no grain.
- **Line.** Almost none. Shapes are edged by a hair-thin lighter or darker rim of their own colour; the only true lines are a black staff and the scallop and curl patterns inside fills. Small circles sit at elbows, knees and wrists as hinges (a28, a29).
- **Palette.** Sky as one smooth vertical gradient, slate blue (#4F6A9B) down to peach (#E3A572) in a28/a29, grey-mauve (#9A93A0) to dusty pink (#C9A79C) in a27. Olive leaf (#8E9B52) on darker olive (#5F6B33), tan trunk (#A89060), sand ground (#CDBFA0) sinking to brown-olive (#6E6240), mauve rosettes (#8E5FA6) with pink centres (#D9A0C8), black (#111111).
- **Light.** None cast. No shadows, no highlights; the gradient is the only light.
- **Staging.** Strict profile, full figure, feet on one narrow strip of repeated rosettes at the bottom edge. Two trees bracket the stage left and right (a28, a29). The wide frame (a27) is a tiny walker in an empty field ringed by fronds.
- **Depth.** By scale and rows only: three rows of identical trees shrinking toward pale flat mountains (a27). No perspective, no haze.
- **Texture.** Ornament instead of texture: leaves as repeated leaflets on curved stems, trunks filled with fish-scale arcs, hair as stacked spirals.

## How it moves
Inference from stills and captions. The hinge circles imply limbs that rotate rigidly about pins, not bend. Between a28 and a29 trees, strip and gradient are identical while a second figure has arrived and an arm has swung out, so the stage is locked and only the pieces on it change, probably by sliding in sideways and swinging limbs in arcs. Nothing suggests squash, blur or camera travel.

## LAW
Through the glass everything is flat cut paper with clean edges and no cast shadow: plain or patterned fills, each shape rimmed by a hair-thin line of its own colour. The water is one smooth gradient, slate blue above sinking to warm peach. Figures stand in strict profile on a narrow ground strip of one repeated round motif, bracketed by two ornamental trees built from repeated leaflets and scale-patterned trunks. Bodies are rigid pieces hinged at visible round pins; a limb swings in a clean arc from its pin and stops dead. The stage never changes; only the pieces on it do.

## Fits
Suits two-party standoffs, toll, border and ownership gags, puppets and signs, anything read as "thing or cutout", and rules where a fixed ground takes a new mark. Does not suit murk, lamp-reveal portholes, soft creatures, fluid or smoke, crowds in depth, or anything needing shadow.
