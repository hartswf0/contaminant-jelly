## How it moves
Inference only; neither still shows blur. The poses (fin raised to the chest, mouth open, pupils leaning in) suggest small puppet-like gestures and talking while the body stays planted. The caustic bands in a45 imply slowly sliding light on the sand. Captions call the board the one readable zone, so the eye is probably held there in long, still shots.

## LAW
Through the glass, the view looks like a small built model filmed under water rather than a drawing: rounded solid forms with no outline, glossy skin, matte cloth and wood, fine sand. Soft daylight falls from above and slides in slow rippled bands across the floor, throwing one long shadow. Focus is shallow, so a blurred mossy stone sits close, the middle is sharp and the far water melts into green haze. One dark slate surface carries the only crisp marks, in thin dusty white chalk. Figures stand planted and make small, deliberate gestures; little else stirs but drifting specks.

## Fits
Suits boards, signs, notices, lessons, queues and audiences, any porthole where one object must be read and small figures react low in frame. Does not suit fast action, big silhouettes meant to stay unreadable, flat graphic gags, or deep wide vistas.
