- **Line.** None; edges come from light. Soft focus, mild grain.
- **Palette.** Slightly faded. Fluorescent white (#E9EEF0), dusk violet (#7C6FB0), slate tile (#4C5560), oxblood (#5A2A2C), bottle green (#2F5A4A), denim (#5F7F96), khaki (#B9AE8C), pegboard cream (#D8D2BE), label squares in pink (#D9608F), orange (#E0702E), green (#4FA83C) and blue (#2E5FB0).

- **Staging.** a43: one figure large at the left edge, cut at the knee; a second small and deep on the right. a44: two in profile at arm's length, a clear bag of pellets between their hands over a counter.

## How it moves
Inference only. The stiff stance in a43 suggests long held poses, the far figure perhaps approaching a little. The raised hand in a44 suggests talk carried by one gesture while the object stays put between them. Probably deadpan, little travel, cuts rather than camera moves.

## LAW
Through the glass, the view looks like a photographed miniature room, not a drawing: real matt cloth, tile and tin, soft grain, slightly faded colour. Cool white light falls from flat panels overhead and violet dusk shows at the back, so things are pale on top and dim below. One figure or object stands large and close at the frame edge while a second waits small and deep behind; or two face each other at arm's length with one object held between them. Behind them sits a tidy grid of small saturated colour squares. Movement is slight and deadpan: a held stare, one slow gesture, a blink.

## Fits
Suits counters, kitchens, shops, service windows, one prop passed or pointed between two parties, and anything funny because nobody reacts. Does not suit murk, silhouettes, flocks, fast action or drawn exaggeration.

## Atlas frames
- **a43**: the near-large, far-small pairing and the cool overhead panels against violet dusk. Not the people or the clock.
- **a44**: two parties at hand distance with the object between them, and the grid of colour squares behind. Not the faces, the badge or any lettering.
