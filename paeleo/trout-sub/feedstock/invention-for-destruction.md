# Feedstock · Invention for Destruction

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Black-and-white photography of built sets and models that are surfaced like old book engravings. Cut-outs, models and a live performer share one picture under the same ruled line.
- **Line.** Fine parallel hatching follows every form: hoops around the oval hull (a09), plank lines on walls and arch ribs (a10), diagonal bands on a wall and stripes on a dress (a11).
- **Palette.** No colour. Soot black (#0B0B0B), charcoal (#2E2E2E), mid grey (#7C7C7A), silver grey (#B9B8B2), chalk white (#EDEBE4).
- **Light.** One pale hard-edged beam cuts diagonally from upper left behind the hull (a09). Elsewhere flat stage light, black surrounds.
- **Staging.** Frontal and side-on. A large form hangs centre under a jagged arch with dark plants as a near wing (a09). Repeating arches funnel to a vanishing point, a small figure low centre (a10). Tall glass cylinders stand as a near left layer beside a figure (a11).
- **Depth.** Stacked flat planes like a toy theatre: black foreground silhouette, hatched middle, paler hatched back.
- **Texture.** Print grain and soft film grain.

## How it moves
Inference from stills and captions only. The layering suggests planes sliding past one another sideways at different speeds, each staying flat. The hull probably glides evenly without bobbing, and the beam sweeps as a single rigid wedge.

## LAW
Through the glass, everything looks like an old steel engraving brought to life: every surface is shaded only by fine parallel ruled lines that follow its form, in soot black, silver grey and chalk white, with no colour except a single tint from the water behind. Build the view as flat layers stacked like a paper toy theatre, a black silhouette wing nearest, hatched shapes behind, paler ones farthest. Things glide sideways or swing on one pivot, evenly, never bending. One pale hard-edged wedge of light crosses the scene. Fine print grain over all.

## Fits
Suits silhouettes and backlit shapes, arches, tunnels, culverts, bars, machines, hulls, gears, cutaways, curtains and frames. Does not suit soft living things whose colour is the clue (leaves, anemones, stained glass), lettering, or loose watery motion.
