# Feedstock · The Rifle’s Spiral

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Photographed miniature: carved and painted flat-sided objects on a small tabletop stage (a36, a34); in a35 the figure looks pencil-shaded.
- **Line.** No ink outline. Cut edges, shallow carved grooves and seams between jointed pieces (a36).
- **Palette.** Black (#0B0C0E), blue-black boards (#1A2230), slate (#3A4048), ash grey (#8E8C86), bone (#B9B0A0), dusty tan floor (#8A7C66), dull ochre backdrop (#8C7A4A). One accent: rose-pink glow (#C8687A) in a35.
- **Light.** One hard side or top light, quick fall-off to black. Light leaks through shapes cut in dark boards and lands as pale patches on the floor (a34, a35).
- **Staging.** One object, centred, side-on or front-on, on a bare floor against black drapes or a dim graded backdrop.
- **Depth.** Shallow lens focus: object sharp, backdrop soft, edges vignetted.
- **Texture.** Matte paint, wood grain, scuffed floor, dust.

## How it moves
Inference only; the stills show no motion. The jointed seams suggest stiff puppet movement: parts hinge at a seam, tilt or open in small steps, then hold. Light probably changes more than the objects do: a glow swells, a patch slides across the floor. Nothing stretches.

## LAW
Through the glass, the view is a photographed tabletop miniature: objects carved from wood and painted matte, with visible grain, seams and shallow cut grooves instead of ink lines. Colour is almost gone: blue-black, slate, ash grey and bone on a dusty tan floor, with one small warm accent allowed. A single hard light rakes from one side and drops quickly into black; light also leaks through shapes cut in dark boards and lands as pale patches on the floor. Focus is shallow. Things move stiffly on hinges in small steps, then hold still.

## Fits
Suits boxes, trunks, doors, lids, shutters, cut-out peepholes, toys, puppets, stage and magic props, anything read first as a dark solid, then by the light leaking out of it. Does not suit subjects whose colour is the clue, flowing water, soft bodies, crowds or fast elastic action.
