## LAW
Through the glass, the view is a handmade stage set under even, soft, shadowless light: everything is built from flat matt shapes of cut card, felt and painted board in saturated turquoise, lilac, lemon yellow, tomato red and bubblegum pink, with clean cut edges and no ink outline. ## Fits
Suits things that open and shut, clap, blink, bounce or call out on a count; household objects that could have a face; anything read by its rhythm. Does not suit murk, silhouettes revealed by a beam, slow drifting things, fine natural texture or deep space.
