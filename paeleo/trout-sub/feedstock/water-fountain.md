## LAW
Through the glass, the view is a handmade stage set under even, soft, shadowless light: everything is built from flat matt shapes of cut card, felt and painted board in saturated turquoise, lilac, lemon yellow, tomato red and bubblegum pink, with clean cut edges and no ink outline. The background is one flat colour crossed by columns of short identical bars. Plain round stuck-on eyes are allowed on objects. Motion lands on a steady beat: a shape snaps to a pose, holds, and snaps back, repeating exactly, and any marks it gives off appear in whole steps, one per beat.

## Fits
Suits things that open and shut, clap, blink, bounce or call out on a count; household objects that could have a face; signals, speakers, toys, lunchboxes, anything read by its rhythm. Does not suit murk, silhouettes revealed by a beam, slow drifting things, fine natural texture (fur, moss, scales as the clue) or subjects that need deep space.
