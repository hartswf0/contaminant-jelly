# Feedstock · One More Time

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Hand-drawn cel animation over airbrushed backgrounds, seen through a soft video transfer: slight blur, bloom and colour bleed at every bright edge.
- **Line.** Thin, clean, even contour on figures and furniture, in dark blue-grey, never black. Faces are a few lines; forms are flat fills with one cool shadow tone and one pale highlight.
- **Palette.** Electric cyan (#3FC4EC) and pale ice blue (#BFE6F2) for rooms and screens; steel blue-grey (#6F8FA6) for structure; acid lime (#C8E81E) for small lamps and target rings; emerald haze (#2FBF5A); violet night (#5A3FB0) and deep indigo (#1B1E5C); skin in flat sky blue (#5FA8DC); one warm accent, orange-gold (#F2A22E), with cream white (#EDEBE0); magenta and pink sparks (#F04FA0).
- **Light.** Everything is lit by its own sources. Panels and windows glow from within; big soft white orbs with wide halos float in the dark (a13); four-point star glints in red, blue and pink sit on the brightest lights (a14).
- **Staging.** A wide, high view of a deep room where curved desks and floor lines all converge on one glowing central window with a concentric ring at its middle (a12). A waist-up figure placed off-centre behind a dark horizontal bar, against pure gradient (a13). A rounded ribbed dome ringed by pools of coloured light, dark silhouettes in front (a14).
- **Depth.** Strong one-point perspective indoors; outdoors, depth by glow alone, far things dissolving into violet haze.
- **Texture.** Smooth airbrush gradients, faint swirls of mist, grid lines on glass.

## How it moves
Inference from stills and captions only. Figures probably move in limited, held poses with a few drawings per action (an open mouth, a lean), while the light does the moving: orbs drifting and swelling, glints twinkling on and off, rings pulsing outward from a centre. The radiating pools in a14 suggest rhythm, lights beating in time rather than at random.

## LAW
Through the glass, draw everything as clean cel animation: thin even dark-blue contours, flat fills, one cool shadow tone, no black. The water behind is a smooth airbrushed gradient from emerald to violet to deep indigo. Nothing is lit from outside; things glow from within in electric cyan and acid lime, with one orange-gold accent. Large soft white orbs with wide halos drift slowly, and small four-point star glints twinkle on the brightest points. Shapes hold their poses while the lights pulse outward in rings, in a steady beat. Soft bloom on every bright edge.

## Fits
Suits portholes about signals, screens, lamps, dials, target rings, distant gatherings, night-time or deep-water subjects, glowing vents and bubbles, and anything read as "machine or party". Does not suit daylit shallows, paper and archive subjects, chalk, rust and mud textures, or gags that depend on gritty material detail.
