# Feedstock · Song of the Sea

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Flat painting that reads as cut paper washed with watercolour; every shape carries speckle and scratch grain (a37, a39).
- **Line.** Almost no dark contour; shapes meet edge to edge. Line is thin and pale: railings (a37), or glowing chalk-like rings, spirals and dot rows (a38).
- **Palette.** Night indigo (#1B2450), slate blue (#3F5C9A), pale ice blue (#BFD6E6), near-black navy (#0B1026), chalk glow (#D8F2E4); warm accents only: wood pink-tan (#C89A84), rust red (#9A3F3A).
- **Light.** Cool, soft, without cast shadows. Light is a paler patch: a faceted pool on the floor and round windows as discs of blue (a37), soft-haloed white orbs (a39).
- **Staging.** Frontal and symmetrical around one centred object (a38), or one big curve sweeping the frame (a37, a39).
- **Depth.** Stacked flat layers, darker at the edges; no vanishing point.

## How it moves
Inference from stills and captions only. The a39 caption says the ribbons "carry motion", so water probably moves as whole bands sliding past each other, not as ripples. The orbs suggest slow floating drift. The marks in a38 probably brighten in sequence. Nothing implies speed.

## LAW
Through the glass everything is flat stacked layers like cut paper washed with watercolour, grainy and speckled, with no dark outlines and no deep perspective. The water is wide rounded bands of night indigo, slate and pale ice blue that slide slowly sideways past one another. Solid things are simple rounded shapes, darkest at the edges of the view. Light never casts shadows; it appears as paler flat patches and as small soft-haloed white orbs that float upward. Fine detail is drawn only as thin chalk-pale dots, rings and curls that brighten one after another. Warm colour is a rare small accent.

## Fits
Suits windows, lanterns and anything that makes light; choirs and sound shown as pattern; night water, chapels, shells, round objects centred in the port. Does not suit daylight shallows, crisp mechanical gags, fast crowds, photo-archive subjects, or lettering.

## Atlas frames
- **a37**: the faceted pale patch on the floor; borrow how light lands. It holds a figure and furniture, so do not pass it.
- **a38**: chalk-pale marks glowing on a dark-framed shape; borrow the mark-making only, the object would leak.
- **a39**: sliding rounded bands of blue and haloed orbs, nothing nameable in it; safest to pass.
