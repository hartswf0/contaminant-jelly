# Feedstock · Pyramid Song

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Soft rendered underwater space, very low contrast, overlaid with thin glowing lines and rows of dots, like a wire sketch not yet filled in (a18).
- **Line.** No ink contour; solids have soft shaded edges. The only line is light: hair-thin, wobbly pale cyan threads, some broken into dots.
- **Palette.** Deep blue-green murk (#0C3A3F to #1A5A5E), near-black teal shadow (#06181B), pale cyan thread (#8FE0DC), grey-green silt (#3F6B68), one pale greenish-white glow (#DDEBD0). No warm colour.
- **Light.** One small source per frame: a soft-haloed spot on the floor (a19), or a lit slab of wall at a doorway (a20). It falls off within a body length.
- **Staging.** Wide, empty, off-centre: tall uprights cross the frame (a18); one line leaves the glow for the dark (a19); a dark near wall hides half the picture (a20).
- **Depth and texture.** Murk, not perspective: far things fade to the water colour. Floating specks, silt haze, soft grain.

## How it moves
Inference from stills and captions only. The specks suggest constant slow drift, like snow in still water. The threads probably sway or draw themselves on point by point. The glow reads as a travelling light that reveals a little at a time. Nothing suggests speed or flashes.

## LAW
Through the glass the water is deep blue-green murk, nearly one colour, full of slowly drifting pale specks. Solid things are soft and outline-free, and only what sits inside one small pool of pale greenish-white light is fully painted; the pool fades within a body length. Everything outside it is drawn only as hair-thin, slightly wavering pale cyan threads and rows of dots that trace its edges, like a room remembered rather than seen. One thread runs on from the light into the dark. Nothing moves fast; things sway, sink and settle.

## Fits
Suits drowned rooms and streets, towns under a reservoir, lamp-toggle portholes where light decides what exists, memory-or-mirage subjects, and ropes or routes leading out of sight. Does not suit bright shallows, crowded gags, crisp character acting, lettering, or warm colour.

## Atlas frames

- **a19**: the small haloed pool of light and the single line leaving it; safest to pass.
- **a20**: light stopping at a doorway edge. Borrow the falloff only; it leaked as a dim photograph in the first p39 take, so do not pass it.
