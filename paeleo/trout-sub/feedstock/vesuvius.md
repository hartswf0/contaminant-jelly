## LAW
Through the glass everything is a hand-made poster: flat, frontal, shadowless, drawn on dusty pink board with matte speckled black, brick red, pencil orange and cream. Shapes are solid blocks with wobbly hand-ruled edges and compass circles, no ink contour, and the main thing stands upright and mirror-symmetrical in the centre like a front-view machine. Marker fills flicker slightly from frame to frame. Around it sit small ruled pencil diagrams, arrows and rows of ticks that are not letters. Parts move like cut paper, in steps: they slide, hinge or pop on whole, and new marks draw themselves on stroke by stroke.

## Fits
Suits charts, blueprints, schematics, signs, totems, home-made machines, prophecy or plan subjects, and lamp-toggle diagrams. Does not suit murk, depth, soft light, swimming animals acted in the round, or portholes that need legible words; the margin marks must stay as ticks.
