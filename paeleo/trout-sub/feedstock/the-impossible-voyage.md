# Feedstock · The Impossible Voyage

Slug: `the-impossible-voyage` · atlas a02, a07, a08

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Black-and-white photographed stage action with colour brushed on by hand. Live performers inside a painted flat.
- **Line.** No ink outline. Edges come from the photograph's darks and painted scenery (rivets, ribs, an arched braced bulkhead); the washes overrun or fall short of them.
- **Palette.** Saffron machinery and lamp beam (#E0A42C to #F08A1E), sea-green water (#2F7F6C, highlights #7FB8A0), dull crimson (#9A2B22), pale jade (#7FAE9A), cream and buff clothes (#E6D5B0, #B89868), grey-brown hull (#6B6253), near-black gaps (#1C1A16).
- **Light.** Flat, frontal, shadowless. Glow is painted, not lit: the orange beam at top right is a wash.
- **Staging.** A side-on cutaway: a long lens-shaped hull with its near wall removed, in compartments. Gold funnel and drum at left, crew room in the middle, door and wheel at right.
- **Depth.** Shallow as a toy theatre: backdrop, one plane of actors, a foreground water band.
- **Texture.** Horizontal brush streaks in the water, grain, soft focus, rounded vignetted corners, unevenly pooled colour.

## How it moves
- Seen: across all three frames the hull, pipes and water band hold their place while the crew regroup around a table. The frame is locked off; only figures change.
- Inference: quick, crowded gesturing in a slightly jerky hand-cranked rhythm.
- Inference: the hand colour shimmers and slips between frames, and the water is a sliding painted band rather than fluid.

## LAW
Through the glass, draw the scene as a hand-painted stage flat seen exactly side-on, with one wall of the thing removed so its insides show as a shallow box, like a toy theatre. Lay colour by hand over grey photographic tone in a few translucent washes: saffron on anything metal or glowing, sea green on water, dull red and cream on small figures, with edges that wobble and never quite fill their shapes. Water is horizontal painted streaks. Machinery is fixed painted scenery; only the small figures inside move, in brisk, slightly jerky hand-cranked steps, with soft flicker and grain.

## Fits
- Suits machines, vehicles and rooms that can be opened as a section, crews at work, and subjects that stay put while small parts move.
- Does not suit single organic silhouettes, open landscapes, or anything needing depth, cast shadow, fluid water or fine line.
