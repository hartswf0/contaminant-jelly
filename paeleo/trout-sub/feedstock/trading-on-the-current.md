## LAW
Through the glass, the view looks like a photographed tabletop miniature under water: no drawn outlines, smooth rounded forms like painted clay with matte skin and fine freckles, resting above a bed of fine even gravel and matte grey stones. Light is soft and diffuse from above with gentle contact shadows, cool teal haze behind, one small warm glint on any metal. Focus is shallow: the main thing is sharp, near stones and far water are soft blur. Motion is slow, weighted and deadpan: a hover, one turn, a blink, a single line of rising bubbles.

## Fits
Suits one small object beside one resident: a bell, a tool, a counter, a trade, anything where weight and a deadpan pause carry the joke. Does not suit flat graphic subjects (maps, grids, lettering), silhouettes or fast elastic motion.
