# Feedstock · Submarines

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Hand-drawn 2D animation like pencil on old paper (a15, a16), plus flat black cut-out shapes in grey murk (a17).
- **Line.** Thin, slightly wobbly dark-brown outline of even weight, no hatching. In a17, thin pale outlines on solid black.
- **Palette.** Sepia paper (#B89A6A), tan highlight (#CDB58A), umber shade (#6E5A3E), dark-brown line (#2E2418). Night: black (#0A0A0A), charcoal (#3A3A38), fog grey (#8C8C88), pale cone (#DCDCD6).
- **Light.** On paper, soft smudged tone and a dark edge vignette. In a17 light is only pale soft-edged cones fanning from a point and fading.
- **Staging.** One large near form fills the frame (hand and round rim in a15, a face in a16) with a small black silhouette inside the round opening. a17 is flat and side-on: one big dark form above, smaller ones below, a spiked ball on a chain at right.
- **Depth.** By size and tone: near things large and outlined, far things small and black.
- **Texture.** Woven paper grain, stains, blotchy rubbed shading.

## How it moves
Inference only. The uneven line suggests outlines that boil faintly from drawing to drawing. Silhouettes probably glide level and slowly, cones sweep as soft wedges, and the grain stays fixed.

## LAW
Through the glass, the view is a pencil drawing on old sepia paper that has been rubbed and handled: thin, slightly wobbly dark-brown outlines, no colour fills, only soft smudged brown-grey shading over a visible woven paper grain with faint stains. Far things are small solid black silhouettes with a few thin pale outline details, hanging in grainy grey murk. Light appears only as pale soft-edged cones that fan out from a point and fade. Motion is slow and level: shapes hang or glide, outlines boil faintly, cones sweep. A dark vignette closes the edges of the glass.

## Fits
Suits things first read as a silhouette and then lit: hanging or tethered objects, beams, small far-off things, faces at the glass, old paper, maps and albums. Does not suit subjects whose colour is the clue (stoplights, stained glass, leaves) or fast elastic motion.

## Atlas frames
- **a15**: a round rim framing a small black shape; paper grain and stains.
- **a16**: the rubbed brown shading and loose single-weight line on a large near form.
- **a17**: pale soft cones in grainy grey, and black shapes carrying thin pale outline details.
