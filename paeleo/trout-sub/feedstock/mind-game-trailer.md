# Feedstock · Mind Game trailer

## What the frames show
- **Medium.** Three registers. a24: a photographed face, grainy and soft, with a thin dark ink contour traced round it. a25: a drawn head of flat angular planes. a26: a rendered long rust-brown structure over water.
- **Line.** Sketchy, broken, blue-black pencil in a25, corners not curves. In a24 one wobbling outline sits on the photograph and does not quite match it.
- **Palette.** Grey-blue skin and cloth (#8FA3AE, #5F7480), near-black hair (#15181C), cobalt and ice blue planes (#1F4FB0, #9CC4F0), white-blue streak (#DCEBFA), rust brown (#6B4A3A), slate sea (#5E7F8C), orange lettering (#F26A1B).
- **Light.** Uneven and flat in a24. In a25 light is a choice of plane: lit facets pale, shadow facets cobalt.
- **Staging.** Extreme close face cropped by the frame (a24); tilted head at centre over horizontal bands (a25); one long mass on a steep diagonal, tipped horizon (a26).
- **Depth and texture.** Shallow. Photo grain against clean flat colour. Large lettering lies over a24 and a25.

## How it moves
Inference from stills and captions only. The horizontal bands in a25 read as speed smear behind a still head: the ground probably rushes while the figure holds. The tilt in a26 suggests a heavy thing falling along its diagonal. The change of medium between frames suggests hard switches, not blends.

## LAW
Through the glass one figure is shown in two materials that never blend. At rest it is a grainy, slightly soft photographed cutout in grey-blue, with a thin dark outline traced loosely around it. When it acts it snaps in a single frame into a flat drawing of angular facets, pale ice blue where lit and cobalt in shadow, sketched in broken dark pencil, while the water behind it turns to straight horizontal white-blue streaks. Then it snaps back. Heavy things sit on one steep diagonal. The switch is a cut, never a fade.

## Fits
Suits substrate-collision and double-take portholes, mirage-or-real subjects, one character doing one sudden thing, weight gags on a diagonal. Does not suit slow drift, crowds, soft light, quiet archive subjects, or objects carrying lettering.
