# What we made and saw together

A record of one long conversation, November 19 to 21, 2026, between a person at the door (Cool Radio) and
Claude. Kept so the next film, and the next session, can start from it. In order.

## What we made
1. **Fifty-five porthole films** from a video model, one per porthole on Turkey's submarine, and an intro.
   The porthole drifted, so every clip was tracked and locked to the same brass rim.
2. **Tourkey Trouttown** (16:35), the classroom film that runs the game, with a narrator, music from
   DEEP SEA VOYAGE, and a number for every child. Worksheets, slips, a class pack, one-sheets, ten song mixes.
3. **A stick-figure explainer.** Thrown out. It left the rule: handmake or forage, no style costumes.
4. **Five hand films** (portholes 01, 03, 14, 35, 44): scissors, chalk, a scalpel, pins, a pen, each working
   with no hand on it. Cut together as **Nobody Is Holding the Chalk** (3:27).
5. **Five class films** (portholes 10, 13, 16, 25, 33) from the pages a fourth-grade class filled in:
   34 sentences, every one kept as the child wrote it, every blank kept blank, the die never thrown.
6. **How to Play the Porthole Game** (6:22), the five class films cut into one with the steps taught.
7. **Five making films**, showing the children at the desk: cut-paper hands, the camera diving into Turkey's
   seat and out into the river. Then **Tourkey Trouttown: The Class Plays** (14:48).
8. **A conference proposal**, four times, and a dissertation chapter outline and blueprint.
9. **Films about how it works:** How a Language Model Makes a Film (2:22, with a lesson and a student
   sheet), Who Held the Chalk (2:57), What Is a SCION? (3:46), Don't Pave the Cow Paths (6:14).
   The last two were called a PowerPoint, rightly.
10. **Behind the Chat** (4:01), the conversation as a scrolling log. Too abstract for a child.
    **Behind the Chat, with Dogs** (1:43), the same told with the dogs of It's All Right.
11. **A cow-path cartoon**, still being made by a swarm running the SCION: three studies so far.
12. **The Dogs of Trouttown**, a thick prompt, not yet run.

## What we saw together
- A row of zigzag teeth in a box, and a second jaw drawn outside the ring. "Little french fries. A new locked school."
- Two fish and a bell, and a speech bubble: "haha verry funny". A friend who wrote three endings for a
  dinner bell, and a next friend who wrote nothing.
- A boxy sub drawn small at the top of the ring. "A giant three-headed monster." "Wave at his twin turkey."
- A fish lying across a bowl with arms and legs. "panit". "by paniting". "It is not wet."
- Tall bars across the whole window. "A ship that sunk." "Trying to save a hot Cheeto." "That's his home." "A movie."
- A porch at dusk, a door, and fifty-four different dogs answering the same three sentences.
- A burned house with four flour sacks in it, and a ledger that says what was not checked.
- Cows on a hill, and the question of where the road should go.

## What happened to us
- The computer restarted in the middle of a swarm. The work was still there.
- Eleven agents ran out of allowance at once. They were the critics and the repairers.
- Claude never heard one film it made.
- The person said no five times in a row near the end, and was right each time.
