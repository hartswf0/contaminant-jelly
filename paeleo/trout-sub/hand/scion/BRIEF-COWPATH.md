# The cartoon: Don't Pave the Cow Paths

The last version of this film was rejected: it was a slide deck. Headings, tables, quotations and captions on
cards, with a narrator reading them. It also claimed to be "animated with" SCIONs that nobody had run.
This one is a CARTOON, and it is made by actually running the SCION.

## What it has to carry, in pictures
1. **The cow path.** Cows walk where walking is easy. Their path gets worn in. A town grows along it. Somebody
   paves the crooked path instead of asking where the road should go.
2. **The one desk.** An animator (a cow) at one desk doing every job in turn (drawing, in-betweening, painting,
   camera, checking), faster and faster, drawings piling up, and approving its own work.
3. **The scion.** A shoot is cut and grafted onto old roots, and takes. (A scion is a grafted shoot. The name
   also honours Anthropic's source-control system Scion, built for agents that are always online; this
   project borrows the name and is not Anthropic's. That goes in the credits, not in the picture.)
4. **The fish.** The work done the other way: a school of fish, each building its own piece at the same time,
   and one fish that built nothing, only watches, and can refuse. A builder is refused, repairs, and passes.
5. **The road.** What gets made when the question "where should the road go?" is finally asked.

## Hard rules
- **It is a cartoon.** Characters with intention: they notice, want, try, meet resistance, and change.
  Every idea above must read with the sound off and no text.
- **Almost no words.** At most 20 words on screen in the whole film, the title included. No headings, lists,
  tables, quotations, labels, captions or diagrams. At most three short spoken lines, or none. Sound and
  music carry it: use the kit's Mix.
- **Run the SCION.** `scion/SCION_AUTONOMOUS_ANIMATION_STUDIO.md` is SCION: AUTONOMOUS ANIMATION STUDIO, in full.
  It is your operating instruction: creative thesis, a bar, the hardest passage first, independent
  criticism, root-cause repair, honest evidence labels. Each scene builder is also handed ONE animation
  SCION whole (`scion/animation-NN.md`) and must work by it: its primitives, its hard proof, its bar.
- **Handmake or forage. No style costumes** (`films/BRIEF-2-oulipo.md`). 960x540, 24 fps, 90 to 130 s,
  under 12 MB. Build with `films/kit.py` (read it in full). The trout, river and table of
  `films/swarm/c13/world.py` may be reused (read its WORLD.md). `cowpaths.py` has a first, weak cow
  silhouette on pins; build a better one.
- Never claim a check that was not done. Nobody here can hear: say so.
