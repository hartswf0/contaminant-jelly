Foraged from Wikimedia Commons, both public domain: Audubon's Wild Turkey (Birds of America, plate 1), cut and hinged into Turkey; Denton's 1896 brook trout plate, every trout. Handmade in code: the pilot's pencil drawing, tin, brass porthole, arch, twig-bell, weed, card, buttons, dice, cup; all sound (kit Mix).
Bolt 1: one pull, two strikes. Bolt 2: a wheel of trout, all clockwise. Bolt 3: each halts in turn, the clapper is held, only Turkey shakes. Bolts 4 to 6: empty lit stage, dull tick. The die stays in its cup.
SCIONs 48, 41, 45.
Weak: captions cross the tin on the table; trout mouths cannot look fed up.
