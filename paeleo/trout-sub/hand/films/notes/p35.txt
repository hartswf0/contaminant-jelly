Foraged: five Farm Security Administration photographs from the Library of Congress (no known restrictions): mailboxes by Rothstein, Vachon and Delano, Lee's Pie Town post office, and Lee's 1940 mail bag, its sack and post scissored out. Trout and Turkey are Wikimedia Commons plates (Denton, Audubon; public domain). Handmade: cork board, typed slips, the blue paper box drawn, cut and brush-numbered, envelopes, pins, green gel, tin, porthole. SCIONs: Library of Congress Print Stage (every print keeps its slip), Physical Tabletop Capture (one timetable drives picture and sound), Secondary Dynamics (sack, door, flag, wattle on damped springs). Weak: no hand holds the tools; sound synthesized; tin rendered, not cut.

Critic: pulled the porthole view back and left so the whole of box #44 sits inside the rim (the "#" was hidden behind the brass); moved the tin 80 px into frame in part three so its ROLLS label and porthole read; lifted the watching trout clear of the caption. Still weak: no hand on pencil, scissors or brush; the intake pipe reads as a vacuum only once letters fly into it; the red flag is small and dim under the gel; the post covers part of the Rothstein slip; prints are 1940-41, not the clue's 1943.
