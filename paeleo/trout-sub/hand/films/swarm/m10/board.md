committed), the `"porthole": 10` entry of `../class/transcripts.json`, row 9 of `../seeds.json`

this sheet, so the film's blank is not a bolt: it is zones 7 and 8, which the sheet came back to the pilot for and
which nobody filled in.

## THE TRACES

Every sign of working that is really on the sheet. "Suggests" means just that. F = front photograph, B = back.

### The pilot (F)

1. **A second jaw, outside the ring.** F, right margin, level with the ring. Inside the ring: a slanted box with four
   tall crossing zigzag teeth, firm. Outside, to the right: a wider, paler drawing of two rows of zigzags meeting
   point to point over a flat line that trails off down and right. Suggests a second go at the same thing where there
   was more room, drawn lighter and faster; which came first cannot be told, the film takes the firm one first.
2. **The bottom of the box is drawn more than once.** F, in the ring, under the teeth: two or three lines fanning
   from the left corner, one pale, one running out past the right side. Suggests the child was not happy with where
   the bottom went and drew it again without rubbing out.
3. **The teeth are pressed, the box is not.** F, in the ring: the zigzags are the darkest marks in the drawing, each
   down-stroke crossing the one before. Suggests the teeth were the point and the box was only somewhere to put them.
4. **Letters far too big for the ruled lines, written over the printed questions.** F, zone 3: every letter is
   about two rules tall. "little" climbs through the words "Who is outside"; "a new locked" sits on top of
   "2. What one thing does Turkey do?"; "it is a Big" runs through "3. How could this use up our air?". Suggests
   the rules were not used as rules at all: the answer went wherever the question was.
5. **No gaps between words.** F, answer 1, first rule: `littlefrenchfries.` is one unbroken run. Suggests it was
   written in one breath, without stopping to think about the next word.
6. **Pressure fades along every line.** F, all three answers: each line starts black on the left ("little", "a new",

7. **A capital E in the middle of two words.** F, answer 1: `frEnch`, `lockEd`. Suggests a child still choosing
   letter shapes one at a time.
8. **A fat round full stop.** F, after "fries": a small drawn circle, gone round twice. Suggests the stop was made
   on purpose: one thing finished, a second thing coming ("a new locked school").
9. **The "w" of "new" is gone over.** F, answer 1, second rule: the middle of "new" is a dark knot, the "w"
   re-traced several times. Suggests a letter that came out wrong and was mended by pressing, not by rubbing out.
10. **Two words, then nothing.** F, answer 2: "freak out" fills a third of the first rule; the second rule is clean.
    The "f" hangs a full rule below the line. Suggests this was the easy one: said at once, and left.
11. **The "B" of "Big" is nearly solid black.** F, answer 3, first rule, directly under "air?": the capital is
    gone over so many times its bowls are filled in. It is the heaviest mark on the whole sheet. Suggests a letter
    changed after it was written (perhaps a small b made into a big one) by going round and round it.
12. **The first word of the second rule is smudged.** F, answer 3, second rule, far left: "so" is grey and
    reworked, the "s" readable as two different letters. Suggests another change of mind made on top of itself.
13. **A small "it", squeezed in.** F, answer 3, second rule, between "so" and "probibly": half the height of
    its neighbours and sitting lower. Suggests it was put in afterwards, when the line was read back and a word was
    missing.
14. **"probibly".** F, answer 3. Spelled by ear. And the word itself is a hedge: suggests the child was not sure of
    the answer and said so.
15. **Nothing on the front is rubbed out.** F, whole page: no scuffed patches, no ghosts. Every correction (9, 11,
    12, 13) is made by adding graphite. Suggests a child who goes forward and presses harder.

## THE FILM (91.5 s)

Seats: **desk** (over the shoulder) / **thought** (inside the thought) / **Turkey's seat** / **river** (looking
back) / **room**. Module lengths: `scene_pilot.SECONDS = 32.0`, `scene_turkey.SECONDS = 25.5`,
`scene_trout.SECONDS = 24.0`, ending (editor) 10.0. Times below are film seconds; a module's own clock is film time
minus its start (32.0 for M., 57.5 for S.).

The desk shot, shared by every scene unit so the cuts match: the written page under
`cam.tilt(desktop, 58, horizon=0.36, zoom=0.9, f=700)` (desktop = `P.desk` with the sheet `P.lay`-ed on it), the
writing hand composited after the tilt at `cam.tilt_point(...)` of the pencil tip, `P.head_back` low in frame
(nape about y 560, so only crown and one shoulder show), and the whole thing through `cam.shoot` with `shake=2.5`
while the child is working and **`shake=0` whenever the child is stuck**. Close work on the words is
`cam.tilt(..., 38, zoom=1.5)` with `shift` chosen to centre the line being written.

Sound cues are for the editor: pencil scratch per letter (`write_plan(...).letters(u)`), a harder, slower scratch
for every "gone over", the rubber once (S.), paper on the passes, a room of children far off under every desk
shot, water whenever we are in a thought or a seat that is not our own, and the room dropping almost to nothing
in the two still holds.

## Notes for the scene units and the editor

- **Words on the page are `wrote`, captions are `reads`.** Lettered with `P.write_on`, never typeset. The pilot:
  seeds 100+, `size` about 2x default, heavy; M.: seeds 200+, default size, round; S.: seeds 300+, default growing
  to 1.3x by bolt 6.
- "container" is spelled correctly by the child; only "probibly" is by ear on the front.
- Each written line must still be on the page in every later beat and in the next module (the sheet carries its
  history: both jaws, the blacked-in B, the ghost "the", the coloured button). Build one `page1(t)` and one
  `page2(t)` function in the film's own time and let all modules call them, so the ending shows the true sheet.
- Every dive returns to the same desk frame it left from (`dive(..., 1 - u)` with the same point), except R4 to R8,
  which goes in by the dashed circle, out through the glass, round, and comes straight back up by the same point.
- Still means still: P9 (4.6 s), T9 (2.0 s), E2 (3.5 s) have `shake=0`, no zoom, no thought ring, no bubbles.
- Thoughts are pictures only. No lettering inside any thought or seat; the brick wall has no sign on it.
- No faces: heads are backs of heads; Turkey and trout are the c10 puppets. No names beyond `M.` and `S.`; the
  pilot's name line is never in a readable frame.
- Passes (T1, R1, E1) are in the incoming module's first half second (E1 in the ending); the editor may instead
  build them from the outgoing module's last frame and the incoming module's `draw(0)`.
