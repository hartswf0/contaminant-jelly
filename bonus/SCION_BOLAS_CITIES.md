# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION: THE BOLAS CITIES (all of Bolas as cities; each cut a school of jellies that rebuilds it)

**The direction:** "Instead of the WYGWYL cities, what if all of the Bolas archive has cities, all videos, and then the aggregate cuts are ecologies of jellies foraging through the city for their films. See the vision." And, while it was being built: "Where are the other tests?"

**Nothing here was watched in motion.** Stills and counts only.

## 1 · The vision, as built
**Every video in Bolas is now a tower, built of its own frames.** There are 434, in seven cities:

| | city | where | films | cuts with a source list |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| b1 | **CRASH LAB** (the 26 catalogued tests: Family, Cineosis, Odyssey, WYGWYL) | a library of stacks | 26 | none |
| b2 | **AFTERLIGHT** | a data center in orbit | 75 | none |
| b3 | **PAELEO** | an underwater data center | 107 | none |
| b4 | **THIS SIDE UP** | a video store the size of a city | 42 | none |
| b5 | **ALLWAYS · ARGON** | a data center on the moon | 97 | **97** |
| b6 | **ALLWAYS · SOL** | an underwater data center | 41 | none |
| b7 | **ALLWAYS · SONNET, OPUS, TENSOR** | a library of stacks | 46 | none |

**"Where are the other tests?"** All of them are here: every Bolas film is in one of these seven cities, and the 26 with test numbers are in CRASH LAB. The fourteen poem cities are still in the menu after these. The earlier experiments (looks, worlds, rooms) are still reached with NEXT EXPERIMENT.

**A film's tower shows the film.** Each floor's windows are real frames from that video, taken at even steps through it. The frames were cut once and stored as one picture per city.

## 2 · Cuts as ecologies: ALLWAYS · ARGON
97 of Bolas's cuts kept an edit list that names where every shot came from: **562 shots from 283 archive films**. Only these can be rebuilt, and in this city they are.

| in Bolas | in the city |
|---|---|
| a cut | **a street** |
| the archive films it took shots from | **source towers** along that street; each floor is one clip it used |
| the cut itself | **its own tower, one floor per shot, in the cut's order**, showing that shot as it appears in the finished film. **It starts dark** |

**What the jellies do:** one cut at a time, one shot a bar.
1. A jelly swims to the source floor of the cut's next shot (ringed, with the source film and year).
2. It lifts the frame and carries it back.
3. The frame is spliced into the reel in the avenue, and **the matching floor of the cut's own tower lights up, edged in gold.**
4. When every floor is lit, the cut is whole. The reel clears and the school moves to the next street.

The card follows it, for example: *cut 3 of 97: "COLLECTIVE INVENTORIES" · shot 6 of 8 · morning record 05 · clearing away (1937)*. The last part is the cut's own note for that shot.

A song is one bar per shot, so a seventeen-bar song rebuilds about two cuts; the city remembers where it had got to, and the next song carries on.

**In the other six cities no film kept a list of its sources.** The jellies there can only forage: they visit floors, come back with frames, and let them go. The card says so, and asks what the city would have needed to keep.

## 4 · What is not yet the full vision
- **One school at a time.** "Ecologies" suggests many cuts foraging at once, crossing where they share a source. Now there is one school, which moves from cut to cut. 61 of the 283 source films are used by more than one cut would be the place they meet; I have not counted that, and it is the next thing to build.
- **Only Argon can be rebuilt.** Afterlight has edit lists in a different form (times within one source), and Sol, Sonnet and Opus have critic records but no shot-by-shot sources that I found. If their sources exist elsewhere, their cities can gain streets like Argon's.
- **Each school looks the same.** A cut should be its own species and colour.
- **The WarGames material and the poem cities are untouched.**
