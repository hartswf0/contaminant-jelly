BONUS · THE JELLY ORCHESTRA

Every jellyfish is one sound

Each soloist is one sampled sound, with a section of moon jellies answering it, in water made of film. Each song is also an experiment: a rule that holds everywhere, and one swerve, where the rule is bent at a single place. Switch the swerve off and you have the control. One animal is ringed: watch that one.
