BONUS · THE JELLY ORCHESTRA

Every jellyfish is one sound

Each soloist here is one sampled sound, and squeezes when its sound plays. Round it swims its section of moon jellies, which answer a moment later, one after another. The water is film: specks of it fall like snow, strips of it hang like kelp, and it shows through every bell. The animals push through it and leave it brighter.
