# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION · THE BODY IS THE VOICE

Where: `bonus/voicelab.html` (the jelly is at the top, always), and TALK in the orchestra.

## 1. Thesis
A voice with no body is a sound effect. Until now the jellies only flashed and squeezed on the beat of each
syllable, so nothing showed that the sound came from them. The voice engine is a physical model of a throat and
mouth. So the animal is now built out of that model: **the bell is the vocal tract.**

## 2. How the body is made
- Fifty times a second the engine reports the open area of the airway at 44 places from the throat to the lips.
- The bell has 44 rings. Ring 1, at the crown, is the throat. Ring 44, the rim, is the lips.
- **When the lips close for m, b or p, the rim pinches shut.** That is how a jellyfish swims. So every closed
  consonant is a stroke, and the body is pushed upward by its own speech.
- The rings follow the airway through springs, so the body lags, overshoots and settles: jelly, not a diagram.
- A light at the crown is the throat. It brightens with the voice, and bands of light run from crown to rim while
  there is sound.
- Fourteen tentacles hang from the rim and are dragged by it.
- At rest it breathes slowly.

## 4. Chinese and Amharic
You said they do not work. I tested whether they run, and I changed what I could reason about. I cannot hear
whether they are right.
- **They run.** In the lab, 妈妈骂马吗 and ሰላም እንዴት ነህ both played with no error, and the rim opened and closed.
- **Mandarin now has tones when spoken.** Before, every syllable was one flat pitch, which for Mandarin is close to
  meaningless. Now each spoken syllable is two pitches: level, rising, low or falling. Measured on 妈 麻 马 骂: the
  pitch held at 440 Hz, rose 294 → 440, fell 261 → 220, and fell 492 → 247. Sung Mandarin still follows the melody.
- **Amharic is unchanged**, apart from the slower speed. Its known faults (ejectives as plain stops, no doubled
  consonants) are still there.
- If "do not work" means something else (nothing plays, wrong characters, a failure on your phone), tell me what
  you see and on which page.

## 5. What was checked
- Lab, phone-sized screen, three languages: rim radius went from about 0.3 (closed) to 1.4 (open) during each
  line; the crown light followed the voice; no errors. Stills show the rim shut on a consonant and open on a vowel.
- Talk, phone and desktop: both jellies visible above the bar; "hello" spoken and answered as 你好.
