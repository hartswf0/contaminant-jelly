# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION · NINE WAYS TO SWIM, ONE JELLY THAT IS YOURS

Where: every world and the game in `bonus/jellyorchestra.html` (Bolas edition `/bolas/cities/`). `?steer=glide` (or
any name below) starts in that scheme.

## 1. What was wrong
The repo already holds a catalogue of steering (`SCION_STEERING.md`: ten schemes in the open water, six rooms in
the Steer Lab). The worlds had one of them, a stick, and it moved a camera. There was nothing to steer and one way
to do it.

## 2. What you steer
**A jelly of your own.** It is the body built from the voice (`SCION_TRACT_JELLY.md`), in teal, and the camera
follows behind it.
- It swims in strokes: the rim closes and the body is thrown forward, as a jellyfish does.
- In the game, it is the jelly that goes through doors and takes reels, not a camera.
- Press it and it gives where you pressed, and sings.

## 3. The nine ways
Press the orange button at lower left. The first press takes the camera; each press after that is the next way.
A line says what the hand does.

| Way | The hand | Borrowed from |
|---|---|---|
| **STICK** | the stick moves; drag the picture to look | the default: something to see and hold |
| **GLIDE** | a finger down anywhere becomes a stick; push to swoop | floating sticks |
| **TAP** | tap where to go; it swims there itself | point and go |
| **DRAG** | pull your jelly back like elastic and let go | slingshots |
| **TWIN** | left thumb moves, right thumb looks | twin-stick games |
| **PADDLE** | swipe up on the left or right to stroke and turn; both to surge; down to brake | rowing |
| **DRAW** | draw a line through the water; it follows | path drawing |
| **WHEEL** | turn the wheel at the foot of the screen; a finger elsewhere climbs | racing |
| **TILT** | tilt the phone to steer; hold a finger to swim | tilt games |

Always there as well: W A S D, Q and E, ▲ ▼, and AUTO to hand the camera back. The choice is remembered.

**Not brought over:** ARMS, the Steer Lab's own scheme where every finger takes a tentacle and hooks things. It
needs things to hook (kelp, stones, posts), which these rooms do not have. The Steer Lab still has it.

## 5. What was checked (phone-sized screen, simulated fingers, in THE ORBIT)
| Way | Gesture | Result |
|---|---|---|
| STICK | pushed the stick up and right | moved 3.0, turned, 3 strokes |
| GLIDE | finger down, pushed up and right | moved 4.9, turned, 3 strokes |
| TAP | tapped a point to the right | turned toward it and swam 3.1 |
| DRAG | pulled the jelly back and let go | flew 3.6 |
| TWIN | left thumb up | moved 3.9 straight |
| PADDLE | swiped up on the left | one stroke, turned right |
| DRAW | drew a line up and right | followed it 6.3, 5 strokes |
| WHEEL | turned the wheel | moved 5.9 and turned |

Also: pressing your own jelly made it sing; in the game, swimming the jelly into the depot door changed the place.
No page errors.

A fault found and fixed while testing: PADDLE had a time limit on the swipe that a slow device would miss.

## 6. What was not checked
- **TILT**: the test machine has no motion sensor. It is written and unrun.
- Real fingers. Two fingers at once (TWIN with both thumbs, WHEEL with a climbing finger, PADDLE's surge).
- How any of these feel. Movement was measured, not judged.
- Sound of the strokes.
