## 2 · Built from the lists of "considered, not built"
| idea (where we discussed it) | what it is now |
|---|---|
| **The other songs** | **a tour.** In the cities, the next song goes to the next city. With PLAY ON, the 50 songs walk the 14 cities in turn, each city in its own architecture: one continuous film. Any song can still be put in any city from the menus |
| **A periodic-table city** (environments) | **a ninth architecture, THE TABLE OF SIGNS.** The 16 by 3 table is drawn on the ground. A shot that has been read as a sign stands on that sign's square. Everything unread stands in a wall round the table. **Gambit:** the archive as far as it has been read. City 01 has 33 read shots among 1,546, so most squares are empty, which is the point |
| **A tide** (gambits) | in the underwater cities and the flooded video-store cities, **the water rises with the song.** Thin bars: ankle deep. Full bars: over the sixth floor. Floors under the surface are dimmer and greener, so the low floors show clearly only in the quiet |
| **Rejected candidates** (archivists) | up to ten foragers per street now **come back holding a frame of their own**, taken from the floor they visited. They hold it up, and near the end of the street it fades: let go. Only the archivist's frame is spliced into the reel |
| **The reel plays** (archivists) | in the last three bars **the reel lifts out of the avenue**, grows, and is held across the middle of the picture: the film the cut made, shown as the ending |
