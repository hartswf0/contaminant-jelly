The Steer Lab

PEEL
  1. Every finger you put down takes one tentacle and keeps it until it lifts. On a rock, the reel, kelp or the film it hooks; in the silt it digs; in open water it braces.
  2. Pull a planted finger toward the jelly to haul on that arm; push it away to pay out. Lift fingers in turn and the bell launches through the gap they leave; the last one down becomes a hinge.
  3. Put a finger back while you tumble to catch yourself. Drag an arm through the silt to rake up the buried gesture: the order you lift it is your edit. Double-tap, or space, to stroke.

The room: one heavy anchor, one loose reel, a hand buried in the silt, and the way out above the far lip. Desktop: hold 1 to 5 to plant fingers where the mouse is; the mouse is a sixth.
