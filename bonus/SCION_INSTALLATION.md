# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION · THE INSTALLATION

Where: `bonus/jellyorchestra.html` (Bolas edition `/bolas/cities/`). `?alone=1` opens with one jelly in a silent room.

## 1. What was asked
No emoji. More controlled, like a museum installation. One jelly that you select, with no particles and no
music. More of a level builder and recorder.

## 2. The controls, in a museum register
- **No emoji and no pictograms.** Every control is a word: RECORD, SEND, HIDE, SAY, MIC, SING, RISE, SINK, CLOSE.
- **One line weight, one colour.** Bone-white hairlines on near-black. No colour coding, no round buttons, no glow.
  A chosen control is filled; nothing else changes.
- **Two typefaces doing two jobs:** small spaced capitals for controls; an italic serif for anything that is
  language (what the jelly says, the caption of where you are, the builder's note), like a wall label.
- The labels over the jellies are an underlined name, not an orange tag.

## 3. ALONE
One button in the ROOM group. It leaves:
- the place, in whatever look you chose;
- your jelly, the one you selected under JELLY;
- its voice, and the words it says;
- anything you built.

It removes the orchestra, the four singers and their singing, the specks, the film strips, the light shafts,
and the music. The choice is remembered and travels in a sent link.

## 4. BUILD
A tray of eight things: plinth, jar, film cans, screen, lamp, column, arch, bench. Press one and it is set down
just in front of your jelly, facing the way the jelly faces. UNDO takes the last one back; CLEAR takes all.
- The screen shows the film the current song is cut from. The lamp is a real light.
- What you build is kept on the device and is written into the link SEND makes, so a friend opens the same room.
- Up to 60 things.

So the sequence is: choose a place and a look, go ALONE, arrange the room, choose the jelly, steer it, give it a
line, RECORD or SEND.

## 5. What was checked (phone-sized screen)
- ALONE in the library: only the room and the chosen jelly were drawn; the music was muted.
- BUILD: five things placed; the tray reported five.
- Ink wash chosen as the look; a line spoken and lit.
- SEND's link, opened fresh, came up alone, with five things, in ink wash, with the same jelly.
- A search of the visible controls found no emoji. No page errors.

A fault found and fixed: in ALONE the four singers were hidden but still singing. They now stop.
A fault I caused and fixed before pushing: a name clash stopped the page from starting.

## 6. What was not checked
- The fix for the singers, and the SAY button's contrast, were made after the last stills and not re-shot.
- A real phone. Sound.
- BUILD has no way to move or turn a thing after it is placed, no snapping, and no walls. It is a first builder.

## 7. Next for the builder
- Pick up, turn and delete a single thing by touching it.
- Named rooms you can save and return to.
- Places for the jelly to be: a mark it swims to on a cue, so a recording can be staged.
