# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION · JELLYFILM, THE SHARED SENTENCE

Where: `bonus/jellyfilm.js`, carried by every page: the orchestra, the loadout, the open water, the three steer
labs, four space, real jelly, the voice lab. It replaces `loadout-button.js`.

## 1. Thesis
Each page kept its own decisions in its own words (`rig`, `pup`, `mode`, `steer`, `look`, `lens`), so a choice made
in one place meant nothing in the next. Jellyfilm is one sentence that all of them hear and speak. A decision made
anywhere arrives everywhere, and each page says back what it heard.

## 2. The sentence
| Word | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| WHO | the jelly | `medusa` |
| HOW | the steering | `glide` |
| WHERE | the place | `store` |
| SEEN | the look | `sumi` |
| SAYS | the words (IN: their language) | `hello` |

- Kept on the device (`cj-loadout`), so it is remembered between pages and between visits.
- Can be written in any address: `sea.html?jf=who:medusa,how:glide,where:store,seen:sumi,says:hello`.

## 3. The rules
1. **Hear.** A page opened without being told a word takes it from the sentence. This happens before the page
   starts, by writing the word into the address in the page's own dialect.
2. **Speak.** A word given in the address is said back into the sentence. So is a choice made inside a page: the
   orchestra's JELLY, STEER, WORLD and LOOK lids, and the open water's own jelly and steering pickers.
3. **Say what was heard.** On arrival each page shows the words it took, for five seconds, at the top.
4. **Never drop a word silently.** A page that cannot draw a word itself gets a lesser form from the shared file:
   SEEN is a colour filter, WHERE tints the page with the place's picture, SAYS is a caption.
5. A mode that runs itself (theatre, the reel game, talk, demo, a shared room) is left alone.
