# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION: TWELVE EXPERIMENTS (each a world, a rule, and one swerve)

**The direction:** "These are great but think more in terms of provocations, test cases and experiments. All these styles are good but need more crisp UI, sound design layers and different approaches to thinking. Consider how Afterlight thinks: each experiment could be a clinamen." With notes on the swerve (Lucretius, Calvino, Bloom, Serres, Deleuze), on Geertz (specificity, thick description, reading over shoulders, model of and model for), "think more handmade", "other worlds, being there, immersion", a system format to model the whole in, and: "take what is useful". Then, while it was being built: **"But also bigger swings at different worlds."**

**Nothing here was heard or watched in motion.** The sound layers were built and run without errors; this machine cannot listen. Only stills were looked at.

## 1 · What was taken, and what it became
| from your notes | what it became in `bonus/jellyorchestra.html` |
|---|---|
| **The clinamen needs a straight line to push against** (Calvino) | every experiment states a **RULE** that holds for every animal, and one **SWERVE**: the rule bent at one place |
| **The swerve off is the control** | **STRAIGHT / SWERVE** switches it while the song plays. Nothing else changes |
| **A test case** | every experiment states a **TEST**: what to conclude if you can, or cannot, tell the difference |
| **Thick description: the wink, not the twitch** (Geertz) | **WATCH** names one small thing: one animal, or one beat of one bar. That animal is **ringed and labelled** |
| **Reading over the shoulders** | **BE THERE** puts the camera just behind the swerving animal, looking at the orchestra over its bell |
| **Clinical interpretation, not predictive law** | **VERDICT** (holds, fails, unsure) is yours, per experiment, per song. It is logged with the song, bar and settings; **LOG** downloads it |
| **Local knowledge; do things that do not scale** | each swerve, sound layer and world is written by hand for that experiment. None is a setting of a shared effect |
| **Other worlds, being there; bigger swings** | each experiment now happens **somewhere else**: its own objects, shape of space, pull and camera (section 3) |
| **Sound design layers** | each experiment has its own layer under the song, played by the same events that move the animals. **SOUND LAYER** switches it |
| **Crisp UI** | one bordered card, top right: number, name, source, RULE, SWERVE, WATCH, TEST, four switches, the verdict. The old cast list is gone. The card folds away on a phone |

## 2 · The twelve experiments
| | experiment | rule | swerve | watch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **DEEP WATER** | every animal squeezes when its sound plays | one moon jelly is always a beat late | the ringed animal answers after its neighbours have finished |
| 2 | **WARM PHOSPHOR** | light fades in about a second | in the last bar of every eight, nothing fades | the exposure fills, then clears on the downbeat |
| 3 | **THE SET** | every cut changes the channel; every channel shows the orchestra | once in eight bars: a channel with nobody on it, only film | bar 6 of each eight, beat 3 |
| 4 | **SILHOUETTE THEATRE** | red belongs to whoever sounds brightest | one animal is red only while silent | red at rest, dark the moment it plays |
| 5 | **WARM CAVE** | sound is heat | one animal plays and stays cold | it squeezes on time and never leaves violet |
| 6 | **LIGHT ALONE** | embers rise | round the bass, they fall | the falling column that follows the largest animal |
| 7 | **MAGIC CIRCLE** | the corps circles the record one way | one circles the other way, three times as fast | it passes every other animal head-on |
| 8 | **SUN IN YOUR EYES** | the eye keeps an afterimage at every cut | once in eight bars: an afterimage and no cut | the colours invert and the camera has not moved |
| 9 | **THE CARD** | everything that plays is engraved | one animal was left out by the engraver | a ring with nothing in it |
| 10 | **THE MEASURED BAY** | everything is on time and on the grid | one animal is 120 ms late, and labelled | the ringed animal against its section |
| 11 | **CUT PAPER** | four sheets; brightness chooses the sheet | on a kick the brightest sheet is a fifth colour, red | the palest bells on each kick |
| 12 | **SPACETIME WORMS** | whatever moves leaves a body of light | one animal does not move at all | a point, where every other animal is a line |

**Each test is a question about you, not the picture.** For example, experiment 5: "Without the ring, would you have found it? If not, heat is carrying the timing, not the squeeze." Experiment 7: "Do the passes fall on beats? They are not made to. If they seem to, you supplied that." The full tests are on the cards.

## 4 · The sound layers
Each is made in the browser, under the song, from the same events as the picture. Each has a quiet bed and voices.
| experiment | bed | voices, and what plays them |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Water | low water | a small rising blip per stroke; a falling tone when the late animal answers |
| Warm Phosphor | a thin high shimmer | a swell on each kick; a faint high note per stroke; air at each cut |
| The Set | mains hum and hiss | static and a thunk at each cut; a tick per stroke |
| Silhouette Theatre | lamp hum | paper rustle per stroke; a soft thud at cuts; a note when the red one plays |
| Warm Cave | a low drone | a sub on each kick; a long drip on snares and hats; a high ping for the cold one |
| Light Alone | fire rumble | crackle on each beat; a low thud on kicks |
| Magic Circle | surface noise | a needle tick per beat; a pitched note per stroke, by the animal's size |
| Sun in Your Eyes | a low hush | a two-part heartbeat on each kick; a thin ringing after each cut |
| The Card | faint scratch | a pen stroke per squeeze, longer for larger animals |
| The Measured Bay | almost nothing | a short beep per stroke; a click per beat; **a double beep, 120 ms apart, for the late one** |
| Cut Paper | faint paper | two snips at each cut; a tap per stroke; a knock on kicks |
| Spacetime Worms | a mid drone | a swell that rises and is cut off, per stroke |

## 5 · The whole system, in your format
```
◻ SPATIOTEMPORAL UNIVERSE   the catalog: 93 recordings, 26 films, the measured moon jelly
→ [Contains]         → ◻ FIELD        one song: sound over bars, film over frames, water over space
→ [Observed Through] → ◻ WINDOW       one experiment
→ [Manifests As]     → ◻ PHENOMENON   the orchestra, as seen and heard

◻ FIELD
→ [Has]      → ◻ DOMAIN        space (x, y, z) × time (bar, beat, sample)
→ [Projects] → ◻ SIGNAL        events: a sound starts, a kick, a beat, a cut, a film frame
→ [Admits]   → ◻ PERTURBATION  the swerve: one rule bent at one animal or one bar

◻ WINDOW   (the experiment)
→ [Defines]   → ◻ OBSERVATION SCALE   WATCH: one animal, or one beat of one bar
→ [Delimits]  → ◻ CONTEXT BOUNDARY    the world: its objects, its shape of space, its pull, its camera
→ [Generates] → ◻ SAMPLING LATTICE    bars and beats; 96 scan lines; four sheets; a grid; a hatch

◻ ENGINE   (the page)
→ [Applies]    → ◻ TRANSFORM       event → stroke → push, glow, sound, light
→ [Constructs] → ◻ REPRESENTATION  soloists, sections, corps, film specks, strips, the look
→ [Optimizes]  → ◻ RECURSION       rule → swerve → test → verdict → the next rule

◻ TRANSFORM
→ [Operates On] → ◻ PATCH        one placement: an exact cut of a recording, at one beat
→ [Produces]    → ◻ INVARIANT    no stroke without a sound behind it; a squeezed bell is narrower and deeper
→ [Requires]    → ◻ CONTRACTION  27% of the stroke: the squeeze

◻ RECURSION
→ [Embeds]   → ◻ MODEL        the RULE: a model of what every animal does
→ [Projects] → ◻ METRIC       the TEST and your VERDICT
→ [Seeks]    → ◻ FIXED POINT  an experiment whose verdict no longer changes from song to song

◻ METAMODEL   (this document and its prompt)
→ [Contains] → ◻ MODEL                 twelve rules
→ [Governs]  → ◻ TRANSFORM HIERARCHY   world > look > swerve > sound layer
→ [Binds]    → ◻ DOMAIN ↔ CODOMAIN     a song's edit map ↔ what you see and hear

Distinctions
1. ◻ MODEL | ◻ REALITY            → [Crossing] → ◻ MEASUREMENT ARTIFACT   the ring and its label: you see the swerve because it is marked
2. ◻ TIME-LIKE | ◻ SPACE-LIKE     → [Crossing] → ◻ CAUSAL CONE            a section answers its soloist in time (a canon) and in space (round it)
3. ◻ CONTINUOUS | ◻ DISCRETE      → [Crossing] → ◻ SAMPLING THRESHOLD     how late is late: 120 ms is labelled, one beat is obvious

Operators
[Observe]  : ◻ FIELD × ◻ WINDOW → ◻ PATCH             a song through an experiment gives one thing to watch
[Encode]   : ◻ PATCH → ◻ SYMBOLIC SPACE               the card: RULE, SWERVE, WATCH, TEST
[Convolve] : ◻ KERNEL × ◻ PATCH → ◻ FEATURE           the look and the sound layer over each event
[Entangle] : ◻ FEATURE × ◻ FEATURE → ◻ RELATION       picture and layer are driven by the same event
[Forget]   : ◻ MODEL → ◻ NOISE FLOOR                  STRAIGHT: the swerve removed, the control
```
**Left out as not useful here:** the closing identity (T ∘ T⁻¹(R) ≡ R ⊗ SCALE). I could not give it a meaning in this system that I could check.

## 7 · As a prompt (for the next experiment)
> 1. **World:** where is this? What is in it, what shape is the space, which way do things drift, where do you stand?
> 2. **Rule:** one sentence that is true of every animal there.
> 3. **Swerve:** bend the rule at one animal or one bar. Do not oppose the rule; tilt it.
> 4. **Watch:** the one small thing to look at, named exactly.
> 5. **Test:** what would you conclude if STRAIGHT and SWERVE looked the same?
> 6. **Sound:** what does this world sound like when nothing plays, and what does one stroke sound like in it?
> 7. Run it on two songs. Log a verdict on each. If the verdicts differ, write down why: that is the finding.
