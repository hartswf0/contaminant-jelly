# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION · THE BLANK ROOM, AND QUIET FIRST

Where: `bonus/jellyorchestra.html` (Bolas edition `/bolas/cities/`). New place `blank`; new default at the entrance.

## 1. Thesis
Less first. The page opened on fifty animals and a song. It now opens on an empty room, one jelly, and no music.
Everything else is something you turn on.

## 2. Why the builder did not work
Things were set down 6.8 below the water's middle, where a floor would be. Most places have no floor, and the
jelly swims about eight above that depth, so what you placed landed out of sight beneath you. There was nothing
to build on and nothing to see.

## 3. The blank room
- A round stone floor 50 across the radius, a dark wall, one light from above. Nothing else.
- Rings are drawn on the floor every eight, so distance can be read. The heavy ring at 48 is where the room ends;
  the current that carries you back starts just inside it.
- You arrive low, near the floor, with the camera looking slightly down.
- It is a place like any other: it is in WORLD, in the loadout's WORLD jar, and can be painted with LOOK.

## 4. Building
1. Open BUILD and choose a thing (plinth, jar, film cans, screen, lamp, column, arch, bench). The chosen one is lit.
2. Tap the floor. The thing is set down where you tapped, facing you.
3. Tap a thing to take it away. UNDO removes the last; CLEAR removes all.
- A tap that misses the floor sets the thing down in front of your jelly.
- In any other place, BUILD offers GO TO THE BLANK ROOM.
- What you build is kept on the device and travels in the link you send, as before.

## 5. Quiet first
- Music is off unless you ask. This covers the song and the cast that sings with it. Your jelly's own voice is not music.
- The entrance asks: QUIET (the default) or WITH MUSIC. The choice is remembered.
- Inside, SONG has MUSIC as its first button in every state, not only when alone.
- `&music=1` in a link turns it on for that visit.
- The song still runs silently underneath, because the orchestra keeps time by it.

## 6. The entrance now opens on
THE BLANK ROOM, QUIET. The Room, The Orchestra, the reel game, Talk, Theatre and Demo are one tap below.

## 7. Checked (headless stills and values read from the page)
- A bare address opened on THE BLANK ROOM. ENTER arrived in the blank room, alone, 2.5 above the floor, BUILD open,
  music off, song muted, cast not singing.
- Four taps on the floor with three kinds chosen left two columns standing: one tap landed on a thing already
  placed and took it away, as designed. Tapping a column removed it. The stick then moved the jelly 2.3.
- No page errors. The first version of the room was overexposed to white; the light was cut to what the still shows.
