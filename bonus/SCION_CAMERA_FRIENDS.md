# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION · THE CAMERA IS YOURS, AND YOU ARE NOT ALONE

Where: `bonus/jellyorchestra.html` (Bolas edition `/bolas/cities/`), two new lids in the puppet column: CAMERA and FRIENDS.

## 1. Thesis
The camera rode behind the jelly and could not be told anything. And a place held one person. Now the camera is a
control like any other, and a place can hold company: jellies that swim with you, and people who arrive as their own.

## 2. CAMERA
| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| BEHIND · FRONT · LEFT · RIGHT | where the camera stands around your jelly |
| ABOVE · LEVEL · BELOW | how high it looks from |
| CLOSER · FURTHER | distance, 3 to 40 (a mouse wheel does the same) |
| WIDER · TIGHTER | the lens, 18° to 95° |
| HOLD | the camera stays where you drag it instead of swinging back |
| RESET | behind, level, 9.5 away, the place's own lens |

- Dragging the picture still looks around, in every scheme that allowed it.
- The tray shows the current distance and lens. The settings are kept on the device.

## 3. FRIENDS
- **ADD A JELLY**: one more body swims with you, up to six, each a different species. They keep a place beside you,
  stroke to catch up, and move their bells when you speak. SEND ONE AWAY removes the last. They are kept on the device.
- **INVITE A FRIEND**: opens a room and shows its link and a QR code. Whoever opens the link arrives in the same
  place as their own jelly (the one in their sentence). Each of you sees the others move. What one says, the others
  hear spoken and see as a line. When one changes the place, it changes for everyone.
- People find each other through the public PeerJS meeting point, then talk directly. Tabs on one device can meet
  without it (`&local=1`).
