# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION: TEN BODIES (every species moves its own way, and three of them in four dimensions)

**The direction:** "None of these are good enough. We need better experimental steering, more different. All species could be more radically different. And also think more about four-dimensional steering."

**Role.** Stop giving every jelly the same controller. Each species gets its own **verb**, its own physics and its own instrument on the glass, taken from what the animal (or its rig) is.

**The test of difference:** if two species could swap controls without anyone noticing, one of them is wrong. Every pair here differs in at least one of these:
- what the finger *is* (a muscle, a threat, a hand on a wheel, a pen, a magnet, a clock hand, a slider into w);
- what the jelly *is* (a jet, a prey animal, a wheel, a drifter, a passenger, a cloud, a history, a slice).

**Where it lives:**
- the page: `bonus/bodies.html?sp=<species>`;
- the Bonus shelf: "Ten Bodies";
- the choose-your-jelly shelf on the index: a verb for every species;
- the 3D lab's card;
- a place in the open water.

## 1 · Why the old steering was not different enough
Every earlier scheme was one of three things:
- a **direction input** (stick, wheel, tilt, twin, tap);
- an **anchor input** (arms, drag);
- a **path input** (draw).

All three tell the jelly *where*. The radical alternatives tell it something else:
- **how hard** (the squeeze);
- **what to fear** (flight);
- **in what order** (a sequence);
- **how to lean** (a wheel);
- **which water to listen to** (layers);
- **what the water should do** (draw the current);
- **where its parts should be** (pour);
- **when** (rewind);
- **which slice of a larger world to be in** (fold, slice).

## 3 · Thinking about four dimensions
A fourth dimension is anything you can move along that is not left–right, up–down or near–far. There are at least six that a jelly could steer in. Three are built; three are next.

| fourth dimension | what it means | built as | the steering it allows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The self's time** (history) | your own past is a line through space, and every point on it is a moment | **REWIND** (the Sentinel) | You go back by touching your trail and sliding along it, and the world's clock goes back with you, so the gates that open and close on a clock are in their old phase again. Letting go starts **a new timeline**. The abandoned one goes on as a **blue ghost** replaying its moves, which you can meet, or touch to **ride your past self**. You steer by choosing which past to continue from. |
| **A fourth spatial direction** (w) | the world is a 4D volume; you see the 3D slice at your w | **FOLD** (the Golden Splat) | A stone is a hypersphere: it grows and shrinks as you move through w. You can *pass beside a stone, in w*, through the place it fills at another w. The lights sit at different w; the slices a little further and a little behind show as faint blue and amber shells, so you can see what is coming before you fold into it. |
| **A film's time** (spacetime) | a film is a block: picture across, time along | **SLICE** (the Film Bell) | The bell's position along the block is the moment. Its heading is the angle of the slice it lights up. Swim straight along the block and the slice is a frame. Swim across it and the slice cuts through time: a **slit-scan**, one picture made of many moments. The bell's own image is the frame it is in. |
| **The world's time** (tempo) | the world's clock is under control: slow, stop, reverse | next | Steer by reversing time: a current becomes its opposite, a fall becomes a lift, a predator un-arrives. |
| **Scale** (semantic zoom) | to zoom in is to move into a smaller world | next | A jelly that steers by shrinking into a film's splats and growing out of them somewhere else (a portal by scale). |
| **Phase** (the beat) | where in a cycle you are | partly: the Glass Moon's resonance | A world that only exists on the beat: walls on the off-beat, gaps on the on-beat. You steer by when, not where. |

**The design rule for 4D.** Four dimensions are only playable if the hidden axis is **surfaced** and **local**:
- **surfaced:**
  - the w strip;
  - the world-time bar;
  - the film timeline;
- **local:**
  - the shells of the next and previous slices;
  - the clock hand on each gate;
  - the slice lit around the bell.

You must always be able to see one step along the fourth axis without taking it.

## 4 · Failures, and how each is designed out
- **Ten skins on one controller.** Each body has its own input topology (hold, poke, band tap, circle, two sliders, stroke, pull, scrub, strip or twist, stick). The one shared stick is used only by the three 4D bodies, whose experiment is elsewhere.
- **The uncontrollable animal** (Flee, Layers, Draw are indirect). Each has an instrument that shows what it will do before it does it:
  - the fear rings;
  - the current column with the drift arrow;
  - the drawn current.
- **The pour that never arrives.** A finger on the plane facing the camera could never pour the veil away from the view, so the veil's and the creature's fingers aim at the jelly's own level instead: higher on the screen is further away.
- **The blown-out spacetime.** Sixty-four frames stacked along the view burned to white. Only the slice is bright; the rest of the block is a faint ghost, and splats near the camera fade out.
