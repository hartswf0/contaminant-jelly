# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION · TWO LANGUAGES AND A DEMO

Where: `bonus/jellyorchestra.html` (Bolas edition `/bolas/cities/`).
Addresses: `?lang=zh` or `?lang=en`; `?demo=1`; both together for a Chinese demo: `?lang=zh&demo=1`.

## 1. Thesis
The work could only be shown to someone who reads English and already knew what to press. Now it reads in
Chinese or English, every sung line is translated, and a demo shows the whole thing in a minute and a half
without anyone touching it.

## 2. Language
- A chip at top right switches between 中文 and EN. A browser set to Chinese starts in Chinese. The choice is kept.
- Translated: the start screen, the dock, the menu and its dials, the worlds picker, the songbook, the game
  (panel, cards, door names), theatre cards, the names of 31 places and paints.
- **The cast still sings in English.** The voice engine's dictionary is English, so it cannot pronounce Chinese.
  In Chinese mode each sung line has a Chinese subtitle under it, and the wall in the Chorus shows both.
- All 50 song titles and the 38 mechanisms were translated by hand for this. They are my translations and have
  not been checked by a Chinese speaker.
- A bar typed in Chinese is refused with a plain message: the cast only knows English words.
- Not translated: the experiment cards (rule, swerve, watch, test), the old full control bar, city names, the
  worlds wall page, and the studio, solo and lab pages.

## 3. The demo
Eight stops of about eleven seconds, interface hidden, one sentence each in both languages (the reader's
language large, the other beneath):
| Stop | Place · paint | Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deep water | Every jellyfish is one sound. 每一只水母是一个声音。 |
| 2 | The Chorus | Four of them sing words. 其中四只会唱出歌词。 |
| 3 | The Orbit · cyanotype | Any place, seen through any paint. 任何地方,都可以换一种画法来看。 |
| 4 | The Film Depot · woodblock | The archive is a place you can walk into. 档案是一个可以走进去的地方。 |
| 5 | The Video Store | Every tape on the shelf is a real film. 架上的每一盘带子都是一部真的影片。 |
| 6 | ALLWAYS · ARGON | Cities built of film. Schools of jellies rebuild the cuts. 用胶片建成的城市。成群的水母把剪辑重新拼起来。 |
| 7 | The Wet Collection · watercolour | The ones in the jars stayed. These are still moving. 罐子里的留下了。这些还在游。 |
| 8 | The Chorus | Write them a bar. Take your turn. Play the game. 给它们写一句。轮到你唱。开始游戏。 |

Start it from the DEMO button on the start screen or in the menu. Double-click the picture to stop it.
