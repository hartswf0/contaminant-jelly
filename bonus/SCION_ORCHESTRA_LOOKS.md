# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION: TWELVE LOOKS (the Jelly Orchestra, after the Bolas experiments)

**The direction:** "Read Bolas Afterlight and the other Bolas experiments and consider more varied aesthetics for each."

**How it was read:** "each" is each song in the orchestra. Until now every song had the same look (dark teal water) and differed only in cast, formation and tint. Each song now takes one of twelve looks, and each look is drawn from a Bolas experiment.

## 1 · What was read in Bolas
`hartswf0/bolas` at its latest revision: the readme and catalog, the Afterlight versions page, each version's note, and the listening room.

**What Afterlight teaches about variety.** Its 25 versions are one concept shown many ways, and each change of look came from a note saying what was wrong with the last. The ten sample studies make it a rule: **each song gets its own picture technique, and the technique is driven by the sound.** That is the rule taken here.

| Bolas experiment | its picture technique (in its own words, shortened) |
|---|---|
| Afterlight 02, The Cold Hand | silhouette theatre: cut-outs on tinted tissue, one red material |
| Afterlight 06, Small Sun, third cut | a rigged cut-paper cast |
| Afterlight 07, Spacetime walk | every clip rebuilt as points that keep the moment they were filmed |
| Afterlight 08, Warm Cave | each clip arrives on a scanline; older frames glow infrared |
| Afterlight 11, Light Alone | only light: a field of embers in wind that the song drives |
| Afterlight 13, Takumi | a 1950s television; every cut changes the channel; the phosphor keeps glowing |
| Afterlight 14, Warm Phosphor | long-exposure light painting; only the lights survive; the exposure turns with each kick |
| Afterlight 16, Magic Circle | the spectrum pressed into a turning record; echoes ripple out |
| Afterlight 17, Warmth, Air, Food, Water | spacetime worms: whatever moves leaves a trail back through time |
| Afterlight 19, Thus the Picture Is Reassembled | thirty-line television: each beat scans another line into place |
| Afterlight 20, Sun in Your Eyes | retinal afterimage; every kick is a blink, red through the eyelid |

## 2 · The twelve looks (as built)
Each look changes four things: the water, what the film specks do, how the animals are lit, and a last pass over the whole picture.

| | look | after | what you see | what the music does to it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **DEEP WATER** | the orchestra's own | dark teal water, light from above, film strips | as before |
| 2 | **WARM PHOSPHOR** | Afterlight 14 | black; every animal leaves a fading trail of light; warm bells | every kick turns the whole picture a little |
| 3 | **THE SET** | Afterlight 13 and 19 | a curved tube, 96 scan lines, one pale phosphor colour, a line sweeping down | every camera cut is a channel change: the picture rolls and fills with snow |
| 4 | **SILHOUETTE THEATRE** | Afterlight 02 and 26 | lit tissue; the animals and the film are dark cut-outs | whatever is sounding brightest turns red |
| 5 | **WARM CAVE** | Afterlight 08 | everything as heat: black, violet, red, orange, white | a squeezing animal runs hot; short trails cool behind it |
| 6 | **LIGHT ALONE** | Afterlight 11 | black; the film is embers, rising in wind; gold bells; no strips | the embers brighten on the beat |
| 7 | **MAGIC CIRCLE** | Afterlight 16 | the film wound into a turning record under the orchestra; the animals in a ring over it | each beat sends a ripple out across the record |
| 8 | **SUN IN YOUR EYES** | Afterlight 20 | ordinary water | each cut leaves a complementary afterimage; a strong kick closes the lid and the light comes red through it |
| 9 | **THE CARD** | Afterlight 26 | cream stock, engraved: more light is more ink, and the strongest light is cross-hatched | a squeeze inks an animal darker |
| 10 | **THE MEASURED BAY** | The Crash-Test Family | a cool grey bay: a floor grid, a back wall grid, orange fiducial crosses | as before |
| 11 | **CUT PAPER** | Small Sun and WYGWYL | four flat sheets of colour with paper grain | brightness steps between sheets, so a squeeze changes an animal's colour |
| 12 | **SPACETIME WORMS** | Afterlight 17 | each animal draws a long body of light behind it | the stroke is visible as a row of bells along the trail |

**How a song gets its look:** in turn, by its number, so neighbours differ. **LOOK** in the bar steps through all twelve on any song; once pressed, your choice stays as the songs change. The placard names the look and where it came from.

**Nothing is copied.** No Bolas image, frame or design is reused. Only the idea of each technique is taken, and it is drawn on jellyfish.

## 3 · Checked
| check | result |
|---|---|
| all twelve looks on one song | no page or shader errors |
| the still test | each look is distinguishable from the others at a glance; animals read as animals in all twelve |
| repaired after the first stills | **THE SET** was mostly noise: more lines, more gain, less snow. **WARM CAVE** flooded the water with orange: a floor was taken off. **CUT PAPER** was mostly one orange sheet: the steps were moved up |

**Not checked:**
- **Motion.** Trails, the channel change, the blink and the kick's turn only exist in motion. Only stills were looked at.
- **Phone speed.** The last pass and the trail buffer add work on every frame.
- **Which look suits which song.** They are dealt in turn, not chosen.

## 4 · Considered, and not built
| idea | from | why not yet |
|---|---|---|
| **Choose the look by the song's mechanism** (the silence song in silhouette, the three-clocks song in the measured bay with the offsets shown as numbers) | Afterlight's rule that the technique answers the sound | needs your taste; in turn is the reversible default |
| **A scoring overlay in the bay**: bell widths per beat, squeeze share, as live numbers beside each animal | The Crash-Test Family | the measures exist in the Real Jelly's CHECK; wiring them to 60 animals is its own job |
| **Spectrogram terrain**: the song's past as ridges behind the orchestra | Afterlight 18 | needs the audio analysed live |
| **A ribbon of the sung line's spectrum**, shedding embers | Afterlight 21 | same |
| **Depth-lifted film**: frames as soft splats in depth | Afterlight 15 and 23 | needs depth for each clip |
| **Drawn animals over graded plates** | The Found Odyssey | a different way of drawing the animals altogether |
| **The seam between two looks burning away into embers** | Afterlight 12, the mix | would suit PLAY ON between songs |

## 5 · As a prompt (for the next look)
> **Source:** a Bolas experiment, and its technique in one sentence in its own words.
> 1. What is the water? What do the film specks do? How are the animals lit? What is the last pass?
> 2. What does the music do to it that it does to no other look? Name the signal: a kick, a cut, the beat, a squeeze.
> 3. Shoot one still on the densest song. Can a stranger tell animals from film from text?
> 4. Put it beside the other looks on one sheet. If it could be mistaken for one of them, it is not a new look.
