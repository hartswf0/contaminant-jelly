# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION · SCHOOLS AND BUILDERS

Where: `bonus/jellyorchestra.html?city=b5` (Bolas edition: `/bolas/cities/?city=b5`). Add `&lens=1`…`11` for a builder.

## 1. Thesis
A Bolas city holds every film. A cut is a list of shots taken from those films. Rebuilding a cut is work, and
work has a style. So: many cuts are rebuilt at once, each by its own species, and each aesthetic builds its own way.

## 2. Schools
- Four schools at once (two on a narrow screen). Each school owns one cut.
- A school is one archivist of its own species (glass, golden, reel, medusa, celluloid, doodle, mantle, sentinel,
  veil, filmbell; no two schools share one) and a share of the moon jellies, tinted the school's colour.
- Each bar the archivist swims to the source floor of its next shot, takes the frame, and delivers it to the cut's
  own tower, which gains a lit floor. The moon jellies of the school forage the same street.
- When a cut is whole the school takes the next cut nobody is working on.
- Every street with a school on it is lit. The card lists the schools: species, cut title, shot k of n.
- Only the Argon city (b5) has shot lists, so only there do schools build. The other six cities forage.

## 3. Builders: one swarm instruction per aesthetic
Each is a rule for how a frame gets from its source to its cut, and what stays behind.

| Lens | Builder | Hand-made gesture | Test | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | THE PORTER | Carried by hand, set into its floor | Can you follow one frame home? | Frames arrive unseen |
| phosphor | THE DRAFTSMAN | Each route is left as a glowing line for 40 s | Does the city show which cuts share a street? | Lines become a tangle |
| set | THE BROADCASTER | No carrying: the frame jumps in three channel changes | Does a jump read as transmission? | Reads as a glitch |
| silhouette | THE PUPPETEER | The frame hangs from a rod and is walked across | Do you see the hand? | Rod lost in the dark |
| cave | THE FIRE KEEPER | The floor it reaches stays hot four times longer | Can you tell new floors from old by heat? | Everything is hot |
| embers | THE BURNER | The frame lights its floor, then rises and shrinks | Does delivery feel like loss? | Looks like a bug |
| circle | THE WINDER | The carrier spirals twice round its path | Does the spiral read as winding a reel? | Reads as dithering |
| sun | THE STARER | Each delivery burns an afterimage of the city | Does the eye keep the cut? | Constant white |
| card | THE PRINTER | Delivered frames are laid in rows of three beside the tower | Can you read the cut as a contact sheet? | Sheet hides the tower |
| bay | THE EDITOR | The marker shows in time, out time and length of each shot | Do the numbers match the cut list? | Numbers nobody reads |
| paper | THE PASTER | Each frame is stuck on its tower at the angle it landed | Does the tower look made by hand? | Looks like noise |
| worms | THE TRAILER | The frame leaves fading copies along its route | Does the route become a strip of film? | Copies clog the street |

The card shows the active builder's rule under BUILDER.

## 4. What was checked
- Headless stills in b5, song 27, lenses none, phosphor, silhouette, card, paper, worms, embers: no page errors.
- Four schools ran at once with four different species; after 30 s, 32 cut floors were lit, and 53 after about two minutes.
- Kept objects counted: phosphor 20 lines, card 20 frames, paper 20 frames, worms 38 copies, embers 16.
- In/out times were added to `cities/cb5.json` for all 562 shots.

## 5. What was not checked
- I cannot watch motion or hear sound. Whether a spiral reads as winding, or a jump as broadcast, is yours to judge.
- Lenses set, cave, circle, sun and bay ran no test of their own; they share the tested code path.
- The live Bolas site and phone speed were not tested.
- In stills the four archivists are hard to tell from the song's soloists. If that holds in motion, they need a mark.
