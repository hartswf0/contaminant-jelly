The Open Water

One sea joins the four bonus pieces. Swim anywhere: over ridges, into the trench, through the kelp. Collect the archive's films as you go, and swim through a ring to enter its piece.

  1. Hold and drag to swim toward your finger; the bell strokes harder the further you reach.
  2. Two fingers, or shift, to boost. Tap, or space, to stroke once.
  3. Keys: W/S forward and back, A/D turn, R/F rise and sink, M the map.
