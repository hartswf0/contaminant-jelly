The Open Water

EXPLORE
  1. Glide: put a finger down anywhere and push: a stick appears under it and you swoop in curves; push to the rim to surge.
  2. Reins: two fingers are your two fins. Lift both to rise, drop both to dive, pull one harder to carve a turn, spread them to surge.
  3. Tap: tap a place or a film and the jelly swims there itself. Ride the bright currents between the places. Scroll, or the right bar, zooms out to the whole sea.
