# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION: THE ARCHIVISTS (the jellies go and find their material; the work moves into Bolas)

**The direction:** "Make the jellies archivists and move it into Bolas, where they go around finding their materials."

**Nothing here was watched in motion or heard.** Stills and counts only.

## 1 · What the jellies now do in a city
A song still walks the city one street every two bars. On each street:

| who | what it does |
|---|---|
| **the moon jellies** (half of them, a different half each street) | leave the avenue and swim to a floor on the lit street. When one reaches its floor, **the floor flashes gold** and a small click sounds: it has looked. Then it swims back |
| **one soloist, the archivist for that street** | swims to **the floor the poem's cut chose** for that scene (ringed, with its film, year and floor). It **takes the frame**: a single frame of film with its sprocket holes lifts off the floor. It carries the frame back under its bell |
| **the reel** | the frame is **spliced into a reel** lying along the avenue. Street by street the reel grows, in the poem's order. The last nine frames show; older ones slide off |

By the end of a song, the reel is the film the poem's cut made, assembled in front of you from the towers it was taken from.

**As an experiment (on the card):**
| | |
|---|---|
| **RULE** | on every street the jellies go into the towers, looking |
| **SWERVE** | one of them comes back carrying a frame: the shot the cut chose |
| **TEST** | before it gets there, which floor do you think it is going to? |
| **the control** | STRAIGHT: they all come back empty, and no film is made |

The performance switch in a city now reads **ARCHIVISTS**; it still steps to TO CAMERA, CYPHER and off.

**A fix this needed:** 34 of the 88 chosen shots lived in another city, because a film is housed where most of its shots were wanted. Each is now **on loan**: a one-floor tower on the street that uses it. Every street has its chosen shot.
