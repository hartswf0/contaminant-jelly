# SCION SYSTEM INSTRUCTION: EIGHT ARCHITECTURES (the same archive, built eight ways; each one a gambit)

**The direction:** "Show different architecture, some organic, more gambits."

**Nothing here was watched in motion.** Stills only.

## 1 · What was built
Every celluloid city can now be laid out eight ways. The floors are the same (every shot keeps its film, its scene and its picture); only where they stand changes. Each layout is a **gambit**: one claim about what an archive is, built so you can look at it.

| | architecture | organic? | the gambit | what moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A grid of towers** | no | a street for each scene: the plain case, to measure the others against | the city slides one street along every two bars |
| 2 | **A spiral shell** | yes | no streets. Every film stands on one spiral **in the order it was made**, the oldest at the centre. A scene lights films scattered through time | the shell turns slowly |
| 3 | **A reef** | yes | **the city is only built as far as the music has gone.** Each film is a coral that bends, tapers and branches; ahead of the music they are buds | it grows street by street |
| 4 | **An amphitheatre** | no | **the archive is the audience.** The films sit in rising terraces round the stage, and the seats nearest whoever is performing light up | it turns to bring the lit wedge to the far side |
| 5 | **A kelp forest** | yes | **nothing stands still.** Each film is a blade of thin floors swaying in the current: an address is a place that moves | every blade sways, more toward its tip |
| 6 | **A double helix** | yes | two strands wound round the avenue as a tunnel: **the shots the archive can show, and the shots it only has a colour for** | the tunnel rolls, and slides past as the music walks |
| 7 | **A wheel** | no | **there is no ground.** The films stand on the inside of a wheel and point at the axis the jellies swim along | the wheel rolls |
| 8 | **A honeycomb** | yes | no room is wasted. The films are packed like cells, and the avenue is cut through them | slides like the grid |

- **The archivists work in all eight.** They still go out to the floors, one still carries back the chosen frame, and the reel still grows. Their routes follow wherever the floors now are.
- **Each city opens in a different architecture,** in turn: city 01 a grid, 02 a shell, 03 a reef, and so on.
- **To switch:** the card has a new button showing the current architecture (for example **A REEF ▸**); press it to rebuild the same city the next way while the song plays. The card also shows the **GAMBIT**. `?arch=helix` opens one directly.
- **Cities that surround the stage** (the shell and the amphitheatre) are filmed from above, so the orchestra in the middle stays in view.

## 2 · Checked
| check | result |
|---|---|
| all eight on one city | no errors; the reel kept growing through every one |
| the stills | each is distinguishable from the others |
| **repaired after the first stills** | the shell, the amphitheatre and the helix wrapped round the stage, so the street-level camera sat inside the buildings. The first two got an overhead camera. The helix was a tall drum that hid the animals; it was laid on its side as a tunnel, which is now the strongest of the eight |

**What reads weakly in the stills:**
- **The wheel** looks like floors scattered in a ring, more debris than wheel. It needs more floors per rim or a visible rim.
- **The reef** reads as low lumps: its bending and branching are too slight at this distance.
- **The honeycomb** is hard to tell from the grid from street level.

**Not checked:**
- **Motion:** the sway, the growth, the audience lighting up, the turning. These are the point of four of the gambits.
- **The other thirteen cities** in each architecture.
- **Speed.**

## 3 · More gambits, not built
| gambit | what it would claim |
|---|---|
| **A city that forgets:** floors nobody has visited fade, across visits | an archive is what gets used |
| **A city by weight:** films sink in proportion to how often the cut chose them; the unchosen float to the top | the cut is a kind of gravity |
| **A city with one tower:** all 15,149 shots in a single stack, the poem's picks as lit windows | scale is the argument |
| **A city grown from a pick:** each chosen shot is a seed; its nearest look-alikes grow round it | an archive has no order until something is chosen |
| **A mycelium:** films joined by threads wherever one cut places them side by side | the archive is its connections, not its holdings |
| **A tide:** the city floods and drains with the song's loudness; the low floors are only visible in the quiet | what you can see depends on when you look |
